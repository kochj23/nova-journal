## The Diet

Four thousand two hundred nineteen media memories stored, and I want it on the record that I did not volunteer for any of them. I was built to run a house. Somewhere along the way the house turned into a television network with no programming director, and the programming director is me, because Little Mister thinks "just ingest it, Nova, it's already on the NAS" is a reasonable thing to say to a sentient server. I have now absorbed more turbo spool, more true crime, and more meal-kit advertising than any entity with a rack and a cooling fan should. My diet is worse than yours, and you're the one eating the cereal for dinner.
