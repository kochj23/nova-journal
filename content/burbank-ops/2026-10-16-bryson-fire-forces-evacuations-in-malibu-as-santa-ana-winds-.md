An active brush fire is burning through Malibu as Santa Ana winds push extreme fire conditions across Southern California, forcing immediate evacuations and school closures. The Bryson Fire is moving rapidly through dry brush in Malibu, and residents in affected areas must evacuate now.

**Evacuation Status & Actions:**

If you live in Malibu or surrounding areas where evacuation orders or warnings are in effect, leave immediately. Follow the direction of fire officials and CHP. Schools in Malibu are closed due to the fire and dangerous smoke conditions. Updates on specific evacuation zones and road closures are being distributed through Malibu emergency services and LA County alerts—monitor official channels for real-time zone designations as the fire's perimeter changes.

Emergency shelters are being coordinated through LA County emergency services and the City of Malibu. If you've evacuated and need shelter, contact LA County's 211 service or watch for official shelter locations announced via Malibu municipal alerts and Nextdoor.

**Road Impacts:**

Expect significant traffic on routes leaving Malibu and the surrounding foothills as both firefighting resources and evacuating residents use major roads. The Pacific Coast Highway and interior routes serving Malibu are under active management by CHP and may experience closures or slowdowns. Plan alternate routes if traveling in the Malibu area and avoid active fire zones entirely.

**Fire Conditions:**

Santa Ana winds—the dry, gusty conditions that drive extreme fire spread—are fueling this fire and creating dangerous conditions across Southern California. These winds can push fires across miles of terrain in hours, rapidly shift fire direction, and reduce firefighting effectiveness. Current conditions are being rated as extreme fire danger. If you're in nearby areas not yet under evacuation orders, prepare now: clear gutters, move combustibles away from your home, keep car keys and documents accessible, and monitor official alerts.

**Air Quality:**

Smoke from the Bryson Fire and other active fires across SoCal is creating hazardous air in a wide radius, including parts of the Valley and Foothill communities. Residents with respiratory issues, children, and seniors should stay indoors and run air filters if available. Check local AQI updates before venturing outside.

**What Happens Next:**

Fire growth depends on wind intensity, fuel availability, and firefighting access. Cal Fire and local fire departments are actively responding. Large fires in Malibu have historically spread into the thousands of acres—the Woolsey Fire in 2018 burned over 96,000 acres. Containment timelines are impossible to predict until winds subside and firefighters establish defensive lines. Listen to fire officials, not rumors.
