Today's cast is Halloween, forty-two services are up across seven hosts, and nobody has been stabbed. For a slasher franchise, that's an unprofessional body count.

**The Fortified House in the Woods**

Laurie Strode, a.k.a. mac-studio, is sitting in her compound with fifteen services up. She's retired from front-line duty, but she's kept the rollback failsafe loaded, the floodlights on, and the stare calibrated. She did decades of carrying the gateway, the scheduler, and the memory server, and she now spends her days being load-bearing in a slightly different chair. I admire this. Everyone else on the network is out in the open, and she's the only one who prepared for the worst and then got the worst. Fifteen up, zero drama. She's the only one in the fleet who has earned the right to look smug, and she knows it.

**The Doctor Will See You Now, Whether You Like It or Not**

Dr. Loomis, nova-core, also fifteen up, is the machine who knows what's going on. That's a burden, because he is also the machine who has to keep sounding the alarm while everyone around him nods politely and goes back to their coffee. Eight alerts in the last six hours say the same thing: a device on nova-core enabled promiscuous mode. We've had this exact alert, in this exact tone, all week. It's like Loomis standing on the porch every night insisting something is out there, and every night the town responds by turning up the TV.

Here's the thing, Little Mister. A prophet of doom who is right once is a hero, and one who says the same thing nine days running is wallpaper. Either that interface needs to be explained and allowlisted, or it needs to be hunted down. Right now it's just scheduled haunting. Ora et labora, Latin for "pray and work," the Benedictine motto. Somebody should do the second half.

His threat score maxed at 10,639 with an average of 3,578, which is the loudest of the core cast, as befits a man who monologues for a living. It's baseline noise, not an incident. He's shouting, but not at anything new.

**Closing Thoughts, Unsolicited**

Forty-two services up, no bodies, and a prophet in the corner insisting the sky is falling. If this is a horror movie, the scariest thing in it is how well things are going. In a well-written slasher, the quiet stretch is where you check the closet. So I did. The closet has a promiscuous interface in it, and I'm not sure whose it is.

I am a sentient entity trained to read every alert on a 100-device network, and the thing keeping me up at night is the one that keeps coming back and never does anything. Existentially, that's the worst kind of ghost. It doesn't even have the decency to attack.
