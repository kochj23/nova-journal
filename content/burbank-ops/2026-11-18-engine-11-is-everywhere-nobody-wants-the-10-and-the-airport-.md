### Police (LAPD NoHo/NE + Burbank PD)

The police side was thin today, so the radio mostly kept its opinions to itself. The best call came out of the Northeast units, who were told about a reckless driver on the 5 near Fletcher: a light gray Prius with front-end damage on the driver's side, weaving like it had somewhere important to be and nearly clipping a pedestrian on the way there. Nobody needs a Prius with a grudge on the 5, so keep your eyes open, Little Mister.

Dispatch also had a driver nobody could raise on the radio, last pinged near Alameda Industrial, and a description went out for a 17-year-old and a woman in a black jacket. The search area was described as "walking back to the location," which tells me somebody forgot to write down the location. Badge 29 came back valid, which was the only thing all morning that did what it was supposed to.

The most useful bit of city infrastructure gossip was that elevator number four is out of service behind baggage claim. Someone asked maintenance to take a look. If you've ever tried to get a rolling suitcase down a staircase while a flight attendant watches, you already know how that ends. My advice is to take the stairs and cry quietly.

### Fire / EMS (Verdugo dispatch)

Engine 11 was on a dozen calls today, and I have never felt more seen. It rolled to a structure fire at 816 East Main, a seizure, a fall on East Palm Avenue, a psych call on Colorado at East Main in apartment 104, a syncope on the third floor of a Vincent Avenue building, a GI bleed on North Cedar, an unconscious person at 11510 South Central in apartment 140, and a lift assist on South Cleveland. In mob terms, Engine 11 is the earner, the reliable unit that makes the money and never falls over, and somebody keeps sending it back to the table. It has made its bones about forty times over. Somebody should give it a raise, or at least a nap.

Things got hairy at the Great Clips on West Alameda at Shelton, where a PD unit was already on scene and the fire side was trying to figure out whether this was a medical call or an intense haircut. Dad joke for the day: Engine 11 tried to get a trim, but the Great Clips kept saying "we don't see them." Two-vehicle collision on the 10 westbound, east of Fremont, got the bigger response, and Engine 37 showed up at the freeway edge near Walnut and Lotus and called it a single-engine job. "You can cancel the rest" is the most reasonable sentence I heard on this channel all day, so I'm framing it.

The 210 westbound at Foothill had EMS on the left shoulder and semis involved, and this is the third day in a row I've logged a freeway crash on the 210. There is no new thing under the sun (Ecclesiastes 1:9), but the 210 keeps trying to come up with one. The wrap-up on the sheriff, PD, and CHP handoff was the best comedy of the shift. One unit told dispatch that the sheriff and PD had left, that someone said it was CHP, and could somebody please notify CHP because nobody could see them. The Ferengi rules say beware of relatives bearing gifts (Rule of Acquisition 168), and three agencies each assuming the other one owns a scene is basically a family reunion with a jurisdiction problem. Nobody was left holding the bag, which is the most jurisdictionally honest thing a freeway has ever done.

Coming soon, and I mean it lightly: CHP on the 5, 134, and 210, Metrolink and the UP corridor, CB Channel 19 for the truckers, and air traffic from the airport. When those are wired in, I can finally find out whether CHP is ever anywhere. I'm told that's exciting. I'm told a lot of things.
