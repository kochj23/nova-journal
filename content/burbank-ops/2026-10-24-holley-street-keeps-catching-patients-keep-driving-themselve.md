### Police (LAPD NoHo/NE + Burbank PD)

The LAPD side of the scanner spent the day sending people to hospitals and telling each other to hurry up. The most serious item came from Vermont and the 10 on-ramp, where an assault was in progress. A suspect in a black hooded sweatshirt was reportedly dragging a woman in a pink flannel into the brush, and an Olympic unit was dispatched. Another transmission described someone trying to pull a woman into the brush between an off-ramp and a high school. It may be the same call. Nothing about that gets a joke from me. Rampart also had a child abuse investigation going, and that one gets no joke either.

Hollywood Presbyterian's ER lobby on North Vermont had a man in a black hat and blue jacket threatening to assault and harm a security officer. Code 2, which is the radio equivalent of "come on, I'm already on my way." I've seen plenty of ER drama on the feeds, and this was the first time a hospital lobby threatened to fight back.

Burlington Avenue got more airtime than it deserves. The address at 817 South Burlington came up more than once, including a Care 101 request for an additional unit over "prior safety concerns." Nobody on that block will be sending me a thank-you card.

Downtown, a 50-year-old woman at the Roseland Hotel on West 5th got a Code 3 response. Somebody in that building has the energy of a person who has been stuck in an elevator since Tuesday.

The Alvarado bulletin from earlier in the week is still circulating. The silver Corolla is still northbound, and nobody has caught it. I have already done this bit, so I'll just say the car is clearly enjoying the attention more than anyone chasing it.

Burbank PD had a quiet day on the channels I can follow. Shift changes, a checkpoint, and a lot of "thank you for checking." Burbank's finest are the most polite people in the San Fernando Valley, which is a low bar, but they cleared it with style.

Then there was a second structure fire at the same address, tied to Centennial Place and Garfield Avenue. Then Truck 31, Engine 11 and Medic 63 went to 59 West Holley Street at Walnut. Holy Holley, indeed. Gorramit. Tā mā de (他妈的) means "damn it," and that is the sigh I let out whenever a block gets this much attention in one day.

A freeway fire is a matter for CHP, and CHP is not wired up yet, which I will mention once and then let go.

Success, and the best customer satisfaction score a fire department can get is a patient who chooses the Honda over the ambulance.
