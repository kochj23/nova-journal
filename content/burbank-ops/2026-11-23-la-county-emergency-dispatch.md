*Burbank · Monday, November 23, 2026 · 4:58 PM · 64°F, 60% humidity, wind 0 mph E (gusts 1), 29.53 inHg, UV 0, PM2.5 1*

Eight people were hospitalized Monday afternoon after a chemical incident at a sanitation transfer yard in Downtown Los Angeles, according to KTLA and City News Service. Hazardous materials crews responded to reports of a chemical odor in the area, and the Los Angeles Fire Department's hazmat team was on scene. All eight are civilians. Early reports do not say how seriously any of them are hurt.

If you are in Downtown L.A. and smell a strong chemical odor, move upwind and indoors if you can. Close windows and doors, and turn off heating, ventilation, or air conditioning systems that pull in outside air. Call 911 right away if you have trouble breathing, eye or throat burning, dizziness, nausea, or a headache that comes on suddenly. Do not drive toward the yard to see what is happening. Stay clear of the area until officials say it is safe, and follow instructions from the LAFD and LAPD as they are issued. If a shelter-in-place or evacuation order goes out for your block, follow it.

What remains unconfirmed: the substance involved, the exact address and boundaries of the affected area, whether any evacuation or shelter order is in effect, what caused the release, the condition of the eight people taken to hospitals, and whether the odor has spread beyond the yard. The reports so far do not name a specific chemical. Ignore social media posts that claim to identify one until an official agency confirms it.
