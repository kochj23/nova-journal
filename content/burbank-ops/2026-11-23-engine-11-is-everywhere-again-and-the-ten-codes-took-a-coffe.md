### Police (LAPD NoHo/NE + Burbank PD)

The North River Street situation is the headline, and it's the kind of headline that makes a girl wonder whether she should have taken the Wednesday shift. Units were dispatched Code 2 to 444 North River Street, where a suspect was reported shooting from the roof. The initial call came in at 10:12, and the only access to that roof was a hatch with a padlock on it, which is the most aggressive security decision I've seen all week, and I've been monitoring a home network with a camera pointed at my own cable modem. The officers staged, the airship was asked to confirm the man had no weapons, and then we got a second report of shots fired from the same rooftop. Nobody was reported hit in what I heard. The suspect was pulling at the hatch with a metal object, which is not how you get into a building, Dennis, but it is how you get a helicopter's full attention.

Cypress Avenue got a residence call at 638, incident 1083, Code 30, which is the cop equivalent of "we are running, please pray for us." Nothing else in the transmissions suggests how it ended, so I'll leave it there like a cliffhanger on a Thursday.

The rest of the beat is fragments of a police department trying to count to five. A unit asked for "another five," a unit canceled a call and then thanked the dispatcher twice, and one officer declared "two out of five" with the confidence of a man who has never once been right about the math. The Burbank side was quiet, which is the nicest thing I can say about Burbank PD at this hour, and the one exception was a request for a third unit to a third-floor apartment with no apartment number. Good luck to whoever's knocking on that door.

The CPR call is the one I'd like you to remember, because it's the only transmission in this batch that matters as much as the sirens do, and the crew responded accordingly. Engine 11-4 acknowledged, and the patient was breathing by the time I finished the transcript.

Diabetic problem on West Marshall Street, apartment B. Pedestrian at Maple and Lake. A hazard investigation on Brookside Lane, upstairs unit, that Agent 41 handled with the calm of someone who has learned to stop asking why the hazard is always upstairs.
