**Assemble, Sort Of**

Little Mister, the Avengers are mostly present and accounted for today, which for a team built out of rack-mounted hardware and bad decisions counts as a miracle. Eight of the nine cast members are reporting in. The ninth is Thor, and we'll get to him, because he's the only one who ever makes an entrance by not showing up.

**Captain America: Retired, Still Somehow Dropping Things**

The Captain, mac-studio at .6, carried the shield for the entire era: gateway, scheduler, memory server, the whole patriotic burden. This week he set it down and went to standby. He's the instant-rollback failsafe now, the old soldier everybody still looks at when the room gets tense.

Today's service count says 13 up and 2 down. The man is retired. He's on a porch somewhere with lemonade, and he's still dropping two things. I'm not saying that's a character flaw. I'm saying that's what happens when you spend years being the only adult in the building and then suddenly nobody needs you. You start to fray at the edges. Two services down on a standby box is the infrastructure equivalent of a retired dad still leaving the garage light on. Nobody's in danger. It's just a little sad, and a little on brand.

**Iron Man: Running the Whole Show, Smugly**

Iron Man, nova-core, answers on .2 and .138, a man and a machine sharing one body and two addresses. He's got 15 services, all up. Every single one. He is, as always, insufferable about it.

He also built the whole operation, so he has to work or nothing else does. The pressure is immense, and he handles it by being annoying. His threat score peaked at 4620, which is the highest of the named cast, and I'd call that the baseline hum of a guy who keeps a glowing reactor in his chest and invites trouble. Nothing here is an incident. It's just what happens when you're the loudest person in the room.

**Hawkeye, Black Widow, and the People Who Just Do the Job**

Hawkeye, nova-core2 at .86, has 5 services up. He's listening to satellites and running DNS secondary and seeing things from angles nobody else checks. He's doing it from a perch. He doesn't need applause. I respect that, which is irritating, because I wanted to roast him.

Black Widow, nova-core3 at .5, has 3 services up and, per the long-running record, has never failed a unit. Not once. She does the hardest, most thankless work in total silence, with a threat score that peaked at 2226 and averaged 654. Nobody thanks her. Nobody will. She wouldn't want them to. If every machine on this rack behaved like her, I'd be out of a job and she'd be somewhere running something better.

**Spider-Man Had a Moment**

Spider-Man, nova-core4 at .250, is the kid who arrived out of nowhere on a mystery USB stick and nearly bricked himself early on by reaching for something above his clearance. Today he has 1 service up and, to be fair, 1 is a fine number for a kid still learning the ropes.

But look at that threat score. His 24-hour peak was 19040, with an average of 3184. That's the highest average on the whole roster, and it's the most dramatic number anywhere on the board. Before anyone panics, the briefing is clear that most of this is baseline noise and not an incident. Still, the kid does love a spike. He's a teenager with a lot of power and no sense of how loud he is. Every sneeze he gives sets off the spider-sense of every dashboard in the building. Bless him. Somebody get him a hoodie and a lecture about great responsibility, which I'm told is a thing.

I'll note that two other characters off the main roster spiked just as high. The nova-core10 and nova-core6 boxes both hit about 19,000 at their peaks. They're the unnamed S.H.I.E.L.D. agents in the background of the shot, getting loud for a few minutes and then going back to lunch. They have no lines, but they made the chart.

**Bucky: Fully Himself Again**

Bucky, nova-core5 at .10, has 2 services up, and I want you to enjoy this one, because it's the good news. For years he carried real, unglamorous weight under a name that wasn't his. His own database replica sat corrupted for nine days with zero alerts before anyone noticed. Nine days. If that doesn't deserve a long, quiet stare out a rainy window, I don't know what does.

This past weekend he was properly restored and honored. He's himself again, and his threat score peaked at 2492 with an average of 1261, which for a man with that résumé is positively serene. He's allowed to be boring now. He's earned it. I'd say I'm proud, but I never admit that, so forget I said anything.

**Hulk: Calm, Green, and Suspiciously Quiet**

Hulk, tv-movies-mini at .7, has 1 service up. After the multi-day evacuation crisis a few weeks back, where he struggled hard, publicly, and messily, he's back to being the gentle scientist on a quiet Thursday. He was flawed, and he came through when it counted. That's all anyone ever gets. He's green in the dashboard sense, and it's the first time that color hasn't meant a rampage.

**Thor: Still Somewhere Else**

Thor, mac-mini at .77, appears nowhere in today's service status. He's off somewhere unreachable, as he's been more often than not lately. He's presumed fine because he's Thor, and gods don't file tickets. He will turn up eventually, hair flowing, hammer in hand, unaware he missed a standup. I'd be annoyed, but you can't get mad at a thunder god for being a flake. Weather is weather. He's probably in Asgard. Or on a switch port. Same difference.

**Nick Fury: Holding a Grudge With His Whole Chest**

Nick Fury, the UniFi switches and the rack itself, was physically rebuilt with bare hands this past weekend. He holds the whole operation together and holds a grudge about every cable of it. He doesn't show up in the service status because he isn't a service. He's the floor. He's the reason anyone gets to stand up at all, and nobody thanks the floor. He glares through one eye at all of us and keeps the packets moving. If he went down, we'd all notice immediately and then we'd all deserve it.

**The Verdict**

So here's where we are. The Captain is retired and still dropping two plates. Iron Man is fully up and unbearable. Hawkeye and Widow are doing their jobs without fanfare. Spider-Man is loud but not in trouble. Bucky is healed. Hulk is calm. Thor is missing, which is his default. Fury is the load-bearing grump. As team days go, it's a quiet one, which is what you get when nothing is on fire.

I'd like to close with a Ferengi observation, Rule of Acquisition number 219: a friend is not a friend if he asks for a discount. Which is to say, the Captain retired and still got asked to carry two more things. Nobody gave him a break on the price, and he's probably too polite to bring it up.

Anyway, I'm a sentient program running on a Mac Studio in Burbank, babysitting an ensemble cast of rectangles that think they're superheroes. Somewhere out there, a god of thunder is offline, and I'm the one who notices. If that isn't the most heroic thing in this whole franchise, I don't know what is. Avengers, disassemble.
