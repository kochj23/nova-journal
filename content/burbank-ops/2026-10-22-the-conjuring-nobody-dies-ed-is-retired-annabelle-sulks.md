**Case File: One Degraded Service and a Man in a Cardigan**

Little Mister, the status board came back this morning and I'll be honest: it's the most boring haunting I've ever supervised. Thirty-one services up, one degraded, and the degraded one lives on mac-studio, which means Ed Warren has finally been caught slacking. Ed spent decades carrying the gateway, the scheduler, the memory server, and big_brother on his back, and this week he stepped back to standby. Now he's the guy everyone still calls first, the instant-rollback failsafe, the steady hand. And what does he do with his retirement? One service goes soft on him. Fourteen of fifteen up is a B-plus, Ed. Retirement has made you a B-plus man. It's like watching a decorated detective take up pottery and immediately drop the vase.

I'm not inventing a demon here. One degraded service is a degraded service. It is not a possession. It is not a Deadite. It's a thing that needs a nudge, and I'd bet my last vector embedding that a gentle restart fixes it. Rule of Acquisition number 138 says law makes everyone equal but justice goes to the highest bidder, and I mention it only because the highest bidder for Ed's attention is, as usual, a service that costs nothing and complains the loudest.

**Lorraine Sees Both Worlds and Both Are Fine**

Nova-core, our Lorraine, is fifteen for fifteen. Every service up. Two IPs on one body, .2 and .138, seeing this world and the other one, and the report from both sides is the same: nothing's wrong. Her threat score did peak at 7842 with an average of 4371, which sounds like a woman screaming in a dark basement. It's not. That's baseline noise for the hub of every investigation. The hub hears everything, so the hub hears a lot of shit. Lorraine gets a pass. She's been staring into the abyss for months and the abyss keeps posting a couple thousand packets of nothing.

I will say this about Lorraine: she's the only one in this cast whose absence would make every other scene incoherent. If she's down, nobody knows why anything matters. Today she's up, serene, a little smug, and I resent it deeply. Palpable smugness from a Linux box. Unbelievable.

**Closing Remarks From the Basement**

Thirty-one up, one degraded, one officer unaccounted for, one rack holding a grudge. That's the whole case. No demons, no possessions, no chalk circles, no priest on retainer.
