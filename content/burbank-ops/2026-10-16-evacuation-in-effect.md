*Burbank · Friday, October 16, 2026 · 10:19 PM · 77°F, 34% humidity, wind 0 mph SE (gusts 2), 29.46 inHg, UV 0, PM2.5 4*

Bryson Canyon Fire in Malibu prompts evacuation of Campus Vernon Kilpatrick — active fire with smoke advisory in effect through Saturday morning.

**EVACUATION IN EFFECT**

The Bryson Canyon Fire in Malibu has triggered an immediate evacuation order for Campus Vernon Kilpatrick. If you are at or near this facility, evacuate now. Follow local law enforcement and fire department instructions. Have your evacuation zones and nearest escape routes identified — do not wait for a second notice.

Malibu residents in fire-prone hillside areas should remain alert for evacuation orders. The Los Angeles County Fire Department and allied agencies are actively responding. Check the LA County Sheriff's Department emergency alert system, your mobile alerts, and local news for real-time evacuation updates. If ordered to leave, go immediately.

**SMOKE ADVISORY — SATURDAY 10 AM**

The South Coast Air Quality Management District (SCAQMD) has issued a wildfire smoke advisory valid through Saturday at 10:00 AM. Smoke from the Bryson Canyon Fire (and the Norte Fire, which is also impacting the region) will degrade air quality across Southern California. If you're sensitive to smoke — asthma, heart conditions, respiratory illness — stay indoors with windows closed and air filtration running. N95 masks help for essential outdoor travel. Kids and elderly folks should especially stay inside until the advisory lifts.

**OTHER FIRES IN THE REGION**

Multiple wildfires are currently burning across Southern California. The Bryson Canyon Fire in Malibu is the one with active evacuations at this moment, but fire conditions across the region remain elevated. Monitor LA County Fire's incident information and KTLA's ongoing wildfire coverage for updates on other active fires. The heat and low humidity are feeding rapid spread — this is not a stable situation.

**ROADS & ACCESS**

Expect traffic and road closures around Malibu and evacuation zones. Avoid the area unless you have a specific evacuation need. Emergency vehicles have priority; clear the roads.

**RESOURCES**

- **LA County Alerts:** lacounty.gov/emergency or sign up for emergency notifications via the County's alert system
- **Malibu/Malibu Lost Hills Sheriff:** Follow official accounts for real-time evacuation orders and reopening information
- **LA County Fire:** Follow lacountyfire.org for incident updates
- **Air Quality:** Check airnow.gov for real-time AQI and smoke forecasts
- **Evacuation Shelters:** The County will announce designated shelters if needed — stay tuned to emergency alerts
