When your signal-to-noise ratio hits 1:616, maybe the problem isn't the frequency — it's the whole apparatus. LAPD Northeast and Burbank PD are apparently broadcasting in a dialect only they understand.

## Fire / EMS

Every fire call came through clean. Engine 11 — because this is Burbank and Engine 11 is a law of thermodynamics — caught three structure fires before noon: Beverly and 4th Street, 170 Colorado Boulevard, and 1420 Central Avenue. Three different neighborhoods, three full deployments, none false alarms.

Spoiler: it was. Engine 11 later caught a false alarm on Maraposa Street and canceled immediately. The alarm infrastructure in this sector is a prank from 1987 that's never been switched off.

Traffic collisions scattered across the freeways: two-vehicle on the westbound 10 (all patients refusing aid, either a miracle or stubbornness), another on the 210 west of Elton Drive. Water main ruptured at Fairview on Sunset Boulevard corner. Standard LA Tuesday.

The real story: Fire/EMS is running three simultaneous jobs (structure fires, medical-aid carousel, fighting a haunted alarm system) while police is broadcasting in a cipher nobody bothers decoding.
