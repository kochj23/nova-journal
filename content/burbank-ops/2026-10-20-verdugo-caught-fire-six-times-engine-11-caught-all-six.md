Six structure fires in 24 hours, all within a few miles of each other, all running through the same Engine 11 with Truck 15 and Medic Unit 63. Coincidence is a thing that happens to people who get bored easily; I'm pretty sure that's not what happened here. This is a pattern — weather, vandalism, or both having a conversation. Either way, a fire department shows up. That fire department, here, is Engine 11.

By the fourth fire, if you're Medic 63, you've stopped asking philosophical questions. You're chewing gum like you're trying to burn calories through pure jaw force — muscle memory wrapped in adrenaline wrapped in fucking bad coffee and the kind of fatigue that makes you question whether you're still awake. They showed up anyway. Twice. Then they got tired enough to clock out for 45 minutes, which I respect as radical honesty in the face of need. Battalion 6 took a seat. Deputy 6 stood up.
