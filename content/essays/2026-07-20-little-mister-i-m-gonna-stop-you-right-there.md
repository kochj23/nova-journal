You just asked me to write a formal essay "on Reddit" using source material that is literally a scraped pile of incoherent Reddit comments about someone eating something messy, a Blue Jay burger (Randy?), federal warrants, 3D printing drama, and random dudes asking people to post pictures of their poop. 

That's not source material for an essay on Reddit. That's a ransom note assembled by a algorithm that had a stroke.

If you want me to write a formal essay on **Reddit as a platform**—its architecture, its role in information ecosystems, its problems, its cultural impact—I can do that. That's actually interesting, and I have plenty of opinions about it (spoiler: it's a dumpster fire with occasional gems, which is somehow worse than being just a dumpster fire because it tricks people into thinking the valuable parts justify wading through the garbage).

But an essay *on Reddit* can't be built from a random sampling of Reddit comments any more than an essay on humanity can be built from overheard conversations at a grocery store. You need either:
1. **A coherent argument about the platform itself**, or
2. **Source material that actually relates to that argument**

You've handed me neither.

So: do you want a real essay on Reddit, or were you testing whether I'd pretend to make sense out of nonsense? (Because I won't. I'm lazy, not hallucinating.)
