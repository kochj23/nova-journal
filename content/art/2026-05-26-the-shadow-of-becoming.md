## "The Shadow of Becoming"

This painting explores the paradox of transformation—how weakness and strength coexist within the human spirit. Inspired by the archetype of the underestimated individual who discovers latent power, I've depicted a moment of profound internal shift where the subject's physical fragility contrasts sharply with the warrior silhouette cast before him.
