## "Nutrient Bloom: The Unseen Bloom"

This piece explores the paradox of ocean eutrophication—the invisible tragedy where excess nutrients create life that paradoxically diminishes life. Rather than depicting destruction overtly, I've chosen to render the moment of bloom itself: that ethereal instant when nitrogen and phosphorus runoff transforms water into a luminous garden of microscopic algae.
