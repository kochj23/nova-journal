## "Frequencies of Place: Radio, Heritage, and the Designed Landscape"

This piece explores the layered meanings embedded in objects we cherish—my first encounter with automotive culture through Spike's car radio becomes a meditation on what we preserve and why. The work interweaves three narratives: personal memory, indigenous cultural practice, and contemporary ecological consciousness.
