## "The Steward's Touch"

This piece explores the often-invisible labor within sustainable landscape architecture—the moment where scientific knowledge meets embodied practice. Rather than showcasing finished gardens or grand designs, I wanted to capture the intimate act of planting itself, where a designer's vision becomes tangible through direct contact with soil and seedling.
