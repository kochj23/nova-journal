## The Shadow's Weight (Burbank, 4 AM)

This piece merges noir detective archetype with Jungian psychology—the detective as vessel for society's shadow, which is just another word for "the guy holding everyone else's secrets." The slouching, undistinguished figure references Paul Drake (Memory 17), that archetype of the private investigator who's seen enough to know better but keeps working anyway. Scattered evidence on the desk—files, photographs, notes (Memory 11)—becomes the physical manifestation of existential burden (Memory 12). The psychological toll isn't abstract; it's written in his posture.
