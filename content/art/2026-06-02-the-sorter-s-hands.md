## "The Sorter's Hands"

This painting explores the profound emotional language of labor—how the human body becomes a narrative of dedication and sacrifice. Inspired by Nordic traditions of fishing communities and the historical interconnection between resource, survival, and identity, I sought to capture an intimate moment often overlooked in art history.
