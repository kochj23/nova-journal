## "The Architecture Remembers Its Animals"

This piece explores the liminal space between constructed human systems and the organic life they displace. Drawing from urban ecology's observation that built environments fundamentally alter animal behavior, I've imagined a building as a living entity—simultaneously architect and victim of its own existence.
