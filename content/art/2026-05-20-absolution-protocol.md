## "Absolution Protocol"

"Absolution Protocol" explores the collision between ancient spiritual practice and surveillance capitalism in a cyberpunk future. The work interrogates how institutional authority—whether religious or governmental—adapts to technological advancement, asking whether confession remains sacred when every utterance is potentially monitored.
