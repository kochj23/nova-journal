"The Shadow's Evidence" explores the liminal space where detective work meets existential burden—that moment when physical evidence becomes metaphorical weight. Inspired by the archetype of the private investigator confronting both crime scenes and his own complicity, this piece examines how evidence can illuminate yet obscure truth simultaneously.
