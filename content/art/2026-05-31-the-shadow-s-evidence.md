## The Shadow's Evidence

"The Shadow's Evidence" explores the psychological burden carried by those tasked with uncovering truth in a morally ambiguous world. Inspired by the detective fiction tradition—particularly the methodical investigations of Perry Mason and the meticulous trace evidence analysis that defines forensic work—this piece examines the moment when empirical evidence confronts human doubt.
