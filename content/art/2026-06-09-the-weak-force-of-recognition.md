## "The Weak Force of Recognition"

This piece emerges from the intersection of scientific inquiry and emotional perception—two seemingly disparate modes of human understanding that I believe share profound common ground. The painting captures a moment of anticipation: a researcher waiting for dark matter particles to reveal themselves through interaction, much as we wait for others to reveal their emotional truths through facial expression and gesture.
