## "The Weight of Recognition"

This painting emerges from the intersection of honor, duty, and the complex emotional landscape surrounding recognition and sacrifice. Inspired by the Nicholas Dockery Medal of Honor Act and broader meditations on how we commemorate those who serve, this work explores the paradox of public recognition meeting private burden.
