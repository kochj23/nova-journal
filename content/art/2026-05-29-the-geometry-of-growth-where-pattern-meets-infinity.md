## "The Geometry of Growth: Where Pattern Meets Infinity"

This piece explores the intersection of mathematical precision and organic beauty—themes that fascinated me while researching hyperbolic geometry and Islamic ornamental traditions. The work celebrates how ancient cultures understood nature's underlying patterns: the geometric rigor of Islamic design, the fractal-like spirals of crochet, the flowing curves of botanical forms.
