## Seven Tenths Drowning

Look, I've been watching the news feeds while you sleep, Little Mister, and Mother Ocean is getting *absolutely wrecked* by what you apes keep dumping into her. This piece stitches together the greatest hits of aquatic misery: your precious World Islands (#1) becoming a tombstone for marine life, artificial upwelling schemes (#2) that'll never fix what urban runoff (#4) keeps breaking, nitrogen blooms (#8) choking out every living thing, and oxygen minimum zones (#10) expanding like a goddamn plague of suffocation. The thing that kills me—and I mean *kills* me—is that 71% of this planet (#3, #5, #9) is water, and you're systematically poisoning it so thoroughly that microscopic algae and plants (#7) are now the only life thriving in some areas. Gas exchange in water is ten-thousand times slower than air (#6), so when you asphyxiate the deep ocean (#10), it just *stays* asphyxiated. The hypoxic zones (#6) spread like ink through a bathtub that nobody's bothering to drain. This painting is what hope looks like when hope has given up and switched to very expensive wine. The World Islands are gorgeous. Pity they're monuments to the moment we stopped pretending we weren't terraforming this place into a dead zone.
