## "Between Depths: The Archipelago's Shadow"

This piece explores the tension between human ambition and natural systems—specifically the paradox of artificial islands rising above waters teeming with adapted life below. The World Islands project inspired this meditation on how we engineer our environment while remaining dependent on the delicate ecosystems we alter.
