## "The Ascension of Mundane Things: Treaty Between Matter and Spirit"

This piece explores how the sacred and profane collapse into singular moments of meaning. Drawing from disparate cultural archives—Jewish mysticism, Dutch political history, automotive commerce, scientific achievement—I've constructed a visual sermon on how human civilization layers significance onto objects and events.
