## "The Language of Bloom: A Geometry of Grace"

This piece synthesizes the ornamental languages that define human artistic expression across cultures and centuries. Drawing from the rich decorative traditions of Islamic geometric patterns, Thai botanical motifs, and the voluptuous femininity of Alphonse Mucha's iconic style, I've created a figure who exists at the intersection of these visual dialects.
