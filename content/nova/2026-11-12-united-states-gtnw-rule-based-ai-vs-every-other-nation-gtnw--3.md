Welcome to the Nova Games tables, where nobody human sat down and the planet burned anyway. In this corner, United States: GTNW rule-based AI, the built-in engine running the Oval Office on Hard. In the other, Every other nation: GTNW rule-based AI, a single NPC bloc representing the entire rest of the world and its collective impulse control. Thirty actions. Roughly one minute of real time. Jan 20, 1961 to Jul 20, 1963, which is two and a half years of Cold War in the time it takes to microwave a burrito. The engine played John F. Kennedy in a sandbox scenario, one turn per month.

The opening was pure diplomacy cosplay. Cuba attacked San Marino, Morocco attacked Cambodia, and the US engine's answer both times was "Condemn and impose sanctions," taking the Secretary of State's advice and blowing off the Secretary of Defense, who wanted to arm the victims with $150M in weapons. A strongly worded letter, folks, the Maginot Line of foreign policy. The engine's own scorecard is funnier: it chose the sanctions option repeatedly, yet the session stats show zero sanctions imposed. Then it attacked Laos itself, and Oman piled on, and Laos spent the rest of the game as the planet's piñata. West Germany, Argentina, Mexico, Israel, Poland and the Netherlands all took a swing. Laos surrendered so often it should have gotten a loyalty card.

San Marino, for the record, surrendered to Cuba by April 1961, and the Americans' rhetorical umbrella did nothing for it. It never mediated a single conflict, and the war log reads like a ledger of small states changing hands.

DEFCON dropped from 5 to 3 by turn 2, hit 2 by turn 3, and finished at 1. Approval jumped 14.5 points in April 1961 because apparently voters love a war. It climbed from a 45 start to a peak of 86.3 in May 1962, then fell 22.4 in a single turn, from 81.8 to 59.4 in July 1962, the worst drop of the run, and ended at 73.9. Newspeak, Orwell's dialect built so you can't think the wrong thought, has a word for this: doubleplusgood. That's 73.9 percent approval with global radiation at 390 and casualties at 104,986,828. The voters are thrilled. The voters are also, statistically, a little smaller in number. Impeachment never got past stage 0, so the Oval Office faced no consequences at all.
