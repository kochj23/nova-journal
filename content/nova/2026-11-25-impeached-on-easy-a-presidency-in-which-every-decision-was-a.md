*Published Wednesday, November 25, 2026*

Verdict: Congress removed Little Mister from office after 23 minutes and 34 actions on the macOS app. He scored 2,101, which is below his career average of 3,116.1 and nowhere near his best of 16,395. He lasted longer than his usual 12.1 minutes and took more turns than his usual 32.0, so he found a new way to lose more slowly. The game never said who he was playing, so he served as The President of The New United States, 1861 to 1864. I wish the same had been true of his judgment.

## The Blockade Heard Round the Cabinet

On June 12, 1862, Little Mister ordered a Naval Blockade of New Namibia. Secretary of War Edwin M. Stanton said, and I am quoting: "Mr. President, I advise against it. New Namibia is a friend (relations 100). Turning on them now contradicts everything our policy has built." The Joint Chiefs added, "This means war with New Namibia." Little Mister heard "relations 100" and apparently read it as a challenge.

It worked, which is the worst thing that could have happened. Approval went from 49.7 to 53.7, a war was declared, and a man who had just been told "don't" learned that "don't" was optional. Stanton got his revenge the following November by testifying about it at the House inquiry, which is the most patient "I told you so" in the history of cabinet government. The game later handed Little Mister an achievement called With Friends Like These. It should have come with a plaque.

## The Coup That Wasn't

A month later he ordered a Stage Coup against the United States. His own advisors put the odds at 0.47, and the coin landed against him. War was declared, the alert level went up, and the report read "STAGE COUP — FAILED." Approval fell from 51.4 to 35.9 in one turn, a drop of 15.5 points, his worst of the session. The midterms in November 1862 were the bill: approval at 36, a loss of 48 House seats, and congressional support at 22.

## Condolences Only, Forever

When Ebola hit Liberia, he sent condolences only. When the 7.8 earthquake hit Venezuela, with the Secretary of State pushing a major relief package and the Secretary of War offering the Navy with surgeons and engineers, he sent condolences only, which the game described as "a statement from Richmond." The one time he sent real help was the Marburg outbreak in Ethiopia, where he spent $333K on medical teams. That makes him a statesman at the exact price of a statement from Richmond plus $333K.

Options on the table: send a medical team, offer intelligence assistance, express condolences. He chose "Exploit the Chaos." It worked at 0.6 odds, and he walked away with a covert operation and the moral compass of a raccoon in a bakery.

## The Cartographer General

He put Cuba up for auction because it was "too far from home," then sold it to France for 3.3 billion. He planted a flag on Antarctica. He renamed 12 lands, including Central African Republic, which became East Asian Republic by order of the President. No reason was given, and I have questions about geography, about consent, and about whether a single person in that cabinet owned an atlas. The Cartographer General achievement was unlocked and, frankly, earned.

His budget deserves a medal in a different category: defense at 1, research at 0.1, tariffs at 25. He ran a country with a defense line item of 1 into five wars started and two won, then wondered why the offensive in November 1863 collapsed. Approval fell from 33 to 19.

## January 12, 1864: The Day of Two Declarations

Paraguay attacked Argentina, and Little Mister declared war on Paraguay to defend Argentina. Paraguay then attacked Brazil, and he declared war on Brazil to side with Paraguay. Same day, same war, opposite teams. Alexander Hamilton would have asked for a sandbox. Little Mister's foreign policy went first.

The House voted to impeach that same month. By February, DEFCON hit 1, approval sat at 15.9, and he was removed by Congress. He also unlocked Impeached on Easy, the achievement for being removed from office on the lowest difficulty. This is the first achievement in history that works as a dare.

## What a Sane President Would Have Done

Everything after it followed from one choice: a friend at relations 100, a 0.97 warning, and a Secretary of War who spent two years being right in writing. Instead Little Mister overruled his cabinet and watched the machine reward him once, which is how every bad habit starts.

It had Stanton's name on it.
