*Published Monday, November 16, 2026*

Little Mister, playing as Donald Trump on the macOS app, just did something unprecedented for his record: he survived two full terms of the presidency, handed over the keys at noon on January 20, 2029, and watched his party's nominee crush Gov. Ruth Calloway 534 to 4 in the electoral vote. The game logged it as a defeat anyway, because apparently "the 22nd Amendment allows no third" counts as losing. The scoreboard says 11,787 points, 48 actions, 11 minutes on Easy in sandbox mode. I say that's the best-adjusted presidency of his career, and the least dangerous.

## The numbers, in context

The first 43 went 34 defeats, 3 victories, 2 quits, 2 abandoned and 2 unfinished, which is a record that makes the Washington Generals look like a dynasty. His average score before today was 2,292.2. His last outing, on November 13, was a 1,402-point defeat in 4.4 minutes. Today he scored more than five times his old average and spent more than twice as long doing it, though 11 minutes is still shorter than his usual 14.2. It did not beat his best score of 16,395, so he gets no banner. He gets a participation ribbon and my grudging nod.

## The case file, such as it is

Zero advisors resigned, zero danger warnings ignored, and the biggest one-turn approval drop on his permanent record is zero. The Ancient Ones demanded a sacrifice and he sent a fruit basket.

All he did was answer crises, 23 of them. I can only itemize eight, but those eight are a pattern. Uganda's leader got assassinated, and he evacuated American citizens with charter flights and Marines rather than take the Secretary of Defense's advice to send forces. Barbados took a Category 4 hurricane and got an airlift and a hospital ship. Somalia flooded and got the same, over the Secretary of State and the Secretary of Health. India got a $7B relief package, Germany got $5B, Venezuela's seized leader got an evacuation, and Ghana's collapsing currency got an emergency IMF loan. Every one of those eight worked. Four of the 23 failed overall, and I can't tell you which, so I'm not inventing a villain.

My read is that he picked a lane and stayed in it: write checks, send planes, save the day. It's also the consistency of a man who figured out the game rewards him for being a boy scout and has no interest in finding out what happens otherwise. "Mostly harmless," says the Hitchhiker's Guide, and that is the most honest summary of this administration I can produce.

## The Ghana alliance, or Rule 277

In February 2028, Ghana's currency lost half its value in a week and he backed the IMF bridge loan with reforms attached. In December of that same year, Ghana and the United States signed an alliance. I won't swear the first caused the second, since the game doesn't say, but I suspect the Ferengi would call it a sound investment and an annoyingly cheap one. Foreign aid in his final budget sits at 0.25, so the man bought his friends on the installment plan.

## What went wrong, to the extent anything did

Ukraine surrendered to Russia on May 20, 2025, four months in. He had armed Ukraine with $3B in weapons, lend-lease style, no troops, which was his Secretary of Defense's call. He passed over the Secretary of State's push to condemn Russia and impose sanctions. The front closed with Russia the winner after 12 battles. My read, and it's only a read, is that half-measures on both sides of that choice bought him the worst of each.

The score card also shows 128,837 casualties and a war-support number of 37, which isn't a trophy. DEFCON dropped to 1 in December 2028, then climbed back to 2 by the end, and I'd call that a gorram heart attack for a man who never gave a hostile order. That tells me the world did the panicking on its own, which is the one thing he couldn't fix with a hospital ship.

## The approval story

He started at 58, rocketed to 89.1 in June 2026 on a 15.3-point jump, and finished at 75.8. The midterms in November 2026 came with approval at 78.8 and 13 House seats. His final congressional support was 71.

## The one call

The call that sank him is a definitional one: the game doesn't have a victory screen for a term-limited president who did nothing but good deeds. If you want the record's fourth victory, Little Mister, the Ukraine call is where I'd go back. Take the sanctions, take the ceasefire, take the interesting option. The next time you sit in that chair, stop being the world's most efficient charity and try being the President.
