*Published Monday, November 23, 2026*

Verdict first: this was the fastest way to get removed from the Oval Office that I've ever watched, and I've watched Little Mister do it 36 other times. On the macOS app, playing as Donald Trump on Easy difficulty in a sandbox scenario, he lasted 4.9 minutes and three actions. Congress removed him on April 20, 2025, the final score was 903, and the Senate trial barely had time to find a parking spot.

## The Context

The man averages 14.6 minutes and 32.8 actions a session. Four days ago, on November 19, he won one: 3,132 points in 50 actions and 13.0 minutes, like a functioning adult. His best is 16,395 and his average is 2,744.6, so 903 isn't even in the same zip code. Coming into today he had 49 sessions on the books: 6 victories, 36 defeats, 3 quits, 2 unfinished, 2 abandoned.

## The Denmark Decision

The centerpiece was a Ground Invasion of Denmark, ordered over the objection of Secretary of Defense Pete Hegseth. Hegseth said: "Mr. President, I advise against it. Denmark is a friend (relations 68). Turning on them now contradicts everything our policy has built." He then pointed out that with approval at 28%, the country had no appetite for it.

The Joint Chiefs offered opening odds of 99% with 769K troops, which is the kind of number that makes a man feel like Napoleon. Secretary of State Marco Rubio warned that Denmark would treat this as war, which is the most polite possible way of saying "this is war." The Vice President was the one who actually mattered: no authorization from Congress, the 60-day War Powers clock starts, and Congress would move to impeach at the next poll.

Little Mister clicked past all of it. Fus Ro Dah, the Dragon Shout from Skyrim meaning Force, Balance, Push, applied to a friendly constitutional monarchy with a relations score of 68. The invasion worked, which is the worst possible outcome, because it teaches the wrong lesson. Approval slid from 28 to 25.9, and the impeachment meter went from 0 to 2 at the next poll, exactly as the Vice President predicted. Denmark, for its trouble, flipped to a Theocracy. Nobody asked for that.

## Crisis Management, Such As It Was

Haiti ran out of fuel, with power plants, water pumps and hospitals shutting down. The Secretary of State offered a $5B relief package and the Secretary of the Treasury offered an emergency IMF loan. Little Mister chose to send condolences only: a statement from the Rose Garden. It worked, per the odds, which only proves the game is more forgiving than the people in it.

Then Russia attacked Ukraine, then Albania, then Belgium, and three times running he picked "Declare war on Russian Federation," passing over sanctions and arming the victims each time. He wanted the full dragon. The war declarations technically worked, but the ticker reported that no ally would join an operation against Russia, so he went to war with the world's other nuclear power alone. DEFCON went from 5 to 3 on the first date, then from 3 to 1 on the next. A Military Exercise aimed at Russia went off in there too, because why not poke the bear with a dress rehearsal.

In fairness, the one defensible call was the rogue Pakistani colonel sitting on 18 warheads. Little Mister chose to neutralize with a military strike at 0.7 odds, and the Secretary of Defense recommended exactly that. It worked. It's also the only time all session an advisor's recommendation and his order lined up, and I suspect that was luck.

## The Collapse

Approval went from 51 to 35.4 in a single turn, a drop of 15.6, then to 30.5, then fell again from 30.5 to 15.3, a drop of 15.2. Impeachment climbed 0, 2, 3, 4. Meanwhile Russia rolled up Europe like a cheap rug: Albania, Bulgaria, the Czech Republic, Denmark, Estonia, Finland, Norway and a long line of others all surrendered with reparations attached. Norway's alone came to $43.6B a year. His own war record was 2 wars won and 0 terms won, with Belarus and Kazakhstan sending his offensives home in defeat. Casualties reached 695,282, the final bill for a game that lasted less time than a microwave burrito.

## What a Sane President Does

Moltke said that no plan of operations extends with any certainty beyond the first contact with the main hostile force. Little Mister's plan didn't survive the first contact with his own Vice President.

Little Mister had every opportunity, 99% odds and a full cabinet of warnings, and the genius stayed buried where it was.
