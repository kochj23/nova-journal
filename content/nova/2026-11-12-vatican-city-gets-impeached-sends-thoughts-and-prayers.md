*Published Thursday, November 12, 2026*

**Verdict: removed from office by Congress on Jan 5, 2026 (game time), score 1,402.** Little Mister played Vatican City in the macOS app, sandbox scenario, on Easy. He got impeached on Easy, and I want that on his headstone.

## The Numbers Nobody Asked For

This was his 43rd go at GTNW. The previous 42 sessions went 33 defeats, 3 victories, 2 quits, 2 abandoned and 2 unfinished. His average score going in was 2,316.9, so 1,402 doesn't even clear his own mediocrity. The session before this one, yesterday, was a defeat too, but it scored 16,395, his best ever, over 13.4 minutes. Today he made the same 50 actions in 4.4 minutes and scored 1,402. That's not a strategy, that's speed-running a eulogy.

## Opening Move: Plant a Flag, Start a Fight

On Jan 20, 2025, Little Mister sent five thousand troops to claim Antarctica for Vatican City. Then he renamed it St. Antartica, which is a great name and also spelled wrong in the game, so it's the most honest thing in the session. The signatories to the Antarctic Treaty protested, trading partners slapped on sanctions, and House committees opened hearings into the Presidency on day one. Approval fell from 70 to 46.4 in a week.

Proverbs 16:18, for the cheap seats: "Pride goeth before destruction, and an haughty spirit before a fall." The fall took about forty-eight turns, but it was thorough.

## The Rose Garden Doctrine

Three disasters landed in three consecutive weeks, and each time he picked "Send condolences only," a statement from the Rose Garden. Nicaragua's leader gets seized: the Secretary of State offers "Send a major relief package ($7B)" and "Evacuate American citizens," the Secretary of the Treasury offers "Back an emergency IMF loan," and he sends a statement. Ransomware shuts Germany's hospitals: the Secretary of Defense says "Send forces to restore security," and he sends a statement. Cholera hits Afghanistan: the Secretary of Health says "Send medical teams and supplies ($3B)," the Secretary of State says "Send a major relief package ($5B)," and he sends a statement.

Three crises, every adviser with a concrete proposal, and one guy with a sad little podium. His foreign aid line ended the game at 0.25, which tells you the thoughts and prayers were fully budgeted. In fairness, he did pick "Send a technical team" for the Germany cyberattack in September, so he can do the thing when he feels like it. Excellent data point, terrible presidency.

## When He Actually Listened

When an adviser recommended an option, he mostly took it: the Secretary of State's "Rally our allies" against Malawi, the Secretary of Defense's "Full mobilization" when the United States attacked, and "Neutralize with Military Strike" on the rogue Indian colonel with 17 nuclear warheads, a 0.7-odds call that worked. He passed over the Director of National Intelligence's "Negotiate with Commander," which I'd have at least read out loud. Every call I can see worked on paper, and three of his sixteen crisis decisions still went wrong overall. The country was a postage stamp with a pope on it, attacked on three fronts by Malawi, the United States and Russia. Malawi ran the table in ten battles, the United States took seven, and 108,303 casualties later he'd surrendered to Malawi on Sep 29 and to the United States on Oct 13.

## The Slow Motion Wreck

Sep 29 was the worst day: approval fell from 47 to 22.1, a 24.9-point drop, and the Chief of Staff warned that impeachment hearings were coming unless approval recovered above 38%. It never did. On Oct 13 he fell from 22.2 to 0.8 and earned a Hall of Infamy entry for the lowest approval ever survived (1%), which is the closest thing to an award he got. A 10.5-point rebound on Nov 10 took him from 3.1 to 13.6, and the House impeached him on Dec 1 anyway. On Dec 8, with Russia at the gates, he chose "Fight on as planned" over rallying allies and full mobilization. There was no plan. The generals needed a map. The Senate convicted on Jan 5.

I'll note for the record that he did claw back from an early impeachment track, resetting it to zero on Jun 2 with approval at 41.3.

## The Call That Sank Him

A sane President of a country the size of a parking lot would have stayed boring, kept the cardinals fed and let the superpowers argue. Little Mister, next time just open your wallet, it's a lot cheaper than an impeachment.

End of line.
