*Published Wednesday, November 11, 2026*

Verdict first, Little Mister: you scored 3,807 as Thomas Jefferson on the macOS app, which is more than double your career average of 1,804.3. You also got removed from office by Congress on Sep 20, 1802. It was a defeat, a clean finish, and 20 actions over about 8 minutes of Nightmare-difficulty sandbox. I'm calling it a moral victory, which is what you call a defeat once the points show up.

**The campaign, such as it was**

Little Mister opened like a man with a mortgage on the whole continent. He bought British North America for $3B and renamed it "America's Hat." Mexico became "America's Pants." Then he claimed unclaimed Antarctica as U.S. territory and renamed it "Everybody loves penguins." That's three territorial acquisitions and zero actual foreign policy. The Founding Fathers wrote a Constitution, and you wrote a toddler's dress-up box.

Reality arrived on schedule. France jumped Britain, Austria jumped France, and DEFCON dropped from 5 to 3 in the first month. By July 1801 it was at 1. You were one bad month from a footnote about Thomas Jefferson starting a nuclear war with a quill. No strikes were launched and no warheads flew, so credit where it's due. It still wasn't peace, because the casualty count ended at 478,792.

**The foreign policy portfolio**

Nine surrender demands were rejected in August 1801. By October, the United States had surrendered to Egypt Eyalet. That's the same month your approval fell from 64.2 to 37, a 27.3-point drop and the worst single turn of the run. My read is the surrender had something to do with it. You went from your peak to a Senate-trial track in one fiscal quarter.

You finished with zero wars started, zero won, and zero covert ops succeeded. The war log shows the U.S. surrendering to Egypt Eyalet again in June 1802 and a third time in September. That's two humiliating-terms headlines, and each one made the Chief of Staff's blood pressure worse. Ecclesiastes 1:9 says there is no new thing under the sun. Apparently that includes losing to the same Ottoman province three times.

**The crisis desk**

You decided 23 crises, and the game scored two of them as failures. Most of the other calls were tame. You condemned Russia for attacking Japan, which was the Secretary of State's recommendation. You told Liechtenstein's neighbors "not our war." You recognized Japan's new regime as "business as usual," passing on aid and a covert-opposition option the Secretary of State had put on the table.

Then came the June 1802 earthquake, a magnitude-7.8 that levelled a major city. Your Private Secretary pitched a full federal relief effort. You chose "Downplay it and move on," with the stated reasoning "Call it overblown; spend nothing." The game scored that call as a success. Approval fell from 37.2 to 14.1 that same month, a 23.2-point drop. I suspect the nation noticed a President calling a city-levelling quake "overblown," but that's my read.

You did it again in August. A landslide buried villages in Spanish Philippines, which asked the world for help. The Secretary of State suggested a $100K relief package. You sent "condolences only," a statement from the Rose Garden. That's two disasters, zero dollars, and a perfect record of Jefferson, Patron Saint of Thoughts and Prayers. Approval was 6.5 by then.

**The budget, a crime scene**

The final books say it all. Defense was set at 1. Social was 1. Research was 0.1. Foreign aid, personal tax, and corporate tax were all zero, and the tariff was 25. That's the Bible talking, not me, though I agree.

**The impeachment, twice**

Impeachment stage hit 3 by December 1801. In January 1802 the Senate acquitted you because the votes to convict fell short of two-thirds. It was a free pass and a clean reset to 0. By August the House voted to impeach. By September approval was 3.2 and the Senate convicted. You were acquitted once and convicted once, which means the Senate was open to persuasion and you weren't.

**How it stacks up**

This was the first of your sessions with a score above your average, and it was shorter than your usual. Eight minutes against a career average of 13.4. Twenty actions against 31.1. Your last session, also on Nov 11, was a defeat at 0 points over 48 actions and 12.0 minutes. The best score is still 9,617, and 3,807 doesn't touch it.

**The call that sank you**

It was the earthquake. Approval was 37.2, below the 38 percent the Chief of Staff wanted to see, but there were still months to claw back. One "spend nothing" call turned a recoverable presidency into a 14.1 and started the impeachment clock again. A sane President mobilizes federal relief, takes the hit on the budget, and tells Congress he's the guy who showed up. Fix the quake and maybe you also fix your approval. Instead you were right about the money and wrong about the country.

You found the sucker. It was Congress, until they noticed.
