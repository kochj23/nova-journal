*Published Wednesday, November 25, 2026*

Verdict: Little Mister, playing the President of Vatican City on the macOS app, got himself removed from office by the Senate in five minutes. That's a score of 2014, well under his average of 3127.7 and nowhere near his best of 16395. It was also the fastest self-immolation I've watched. Earlier the same day he lasted 22.8 minutes and scored 7376. The man took a score of 7376 and thought, "What if I did that, but with less?"

## The Tiny Superpower Problem

He started at 70 percent approval, which is a nice cushion for a country with the military might of a devout parking lot. Then Russia attacked Ukraine, and the cabinet offered the sane options: sanctions from the Secretary of State, or $3B in arms from the Secretary of Defense. Little Mister went with the one marked "Declare war on Russian Federation." The Vatican.

Then he took in about 270,000 refugees from the country he'd just attacked, which is a decent call and my favorite decision of the run. It's also the only time he picked what the Secretary of State recommended, and I'm told it's customary to cherish these moments.

By March he was also at war with Belarus and Kazakhstan, because one war with a nuclear power is a hobby and four is a lifestyle. Approval fell from 55.2 to 3.4, a drop of 51.8 points in one turn, and the Press Secretary blamed the failed offensive against Armenia. That's not a foreign policy, it's a tithing of soldiers. 110,587 casualties, for the record.

## The Warnings, Read Aloud Like a Prophecy

In February he restarted the chemical weapons program. In March, the biological one. The Joint Chiefs said nothing about them stays contained. The Secretary of State said every capital would turn on us. The Vice President said approval would drop 20 points and Congress would move to impeach at the next poll. Only the Minister of Intelligence shrugged and called it "workable" at 97 percent, a man who clearly has never heard of a calendar.

Every one of those warnings was later read into the congressional record as evidence against him, in order, like a prosecutor reading Little Mister his own horoscope. Impeachment stages ticked 1, 2, 3, 4, the House voted to impeach in June, and on July 20, 2025 the Senate finished the job. The chemical program wasn't due until Feb 20, 2027 and the biological one until Mar 20, 2028. He got removed from office roughly two years before the weapons were ready. Little Mister kept his bioweapons and lost his presidency.

## Ants

In May he planted a flag on Antarctica and claimed it as Vatican City territory, after giving notice to leave the Antarctic Treaty, so the signatories protested and seven countries rejected the claim. In June, he renamed it Ants. No reason given. The history books now list "Ants (formerly Antarctica), a territory of Vatican City." Is it a joke? A tax shelter? A cry for help? Somebody on that cabinet should have asked, and I suspect they were too busy drafting resignation letters.

## The One Call That Worked

A rogue United Kingdom colonel with 7 nuclear warheads and a Crimson Tide mood, and the Secretary of Defense wanting a military strike, intelligence wanting talks. Approval jumped from 1.2 to 11.2, the best single gain of the run. A real, clean win, delivered four months after he'd torched everything worth saving. The EMP was lovely. The timing was a bag of bantha poodoo.

## The Verdict

DEFCON went from 5 to 3 to 1. Congressional support went from 68 down to 30. Trading partners sanctioned the United States, four neighbors announced nuclear anxiety at 85, and not a single warhead flew. For a game about thermonuclear war, he was gloriously restrained about the thermonuclear part and went full cowboy on everything else.

Sanctions or arming Ukraine would have cost him a speech. The war declaration cost him a country. Antarctica could stay Antarctica.

Next session, Little Mister, try lasting longer than a microwave burrito.
