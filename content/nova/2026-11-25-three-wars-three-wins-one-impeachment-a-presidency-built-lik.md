*Published Wednesday, November 25, 2026*

Little Mister, playing as Jimmy Carter on the macOS app, went 3 wars and 3 wins and still got fired by Congress on 1978-12-20. That is a hell of a trick. The game handed him an achievement called "Impeached on Easy," described as "Removed by Congress on the easiest difficulty." The game is not being subtle.

The session ran 23 minutes and 23 actions. His average is 17.0 minutes and 31.5 actions, so he was slower and more decisive than usual, a bad combination for a man who ends up removed from office. The score was 7376. His record coming in was 7 victories and 37 defeats, so this lands in the usual column. His previous session was a victory worth 14066 in 48 actions and 158.6 minutes. That's a man who stayed up all night to win, then followed it with a 23-minute speedrun to the unemployment line.

## The Opening, Such As It Was

He started by putting troops into Arizona. In 1977. No stated reason, no sign of an invasion. A sane aide asks what Arizona did, and I'll tell you: nothing.

Then he declared war on Israel in June 1977, and again in July 1977, in case the first one didn't take. Approval sat at 58 and DEFCON slid from 5 to 2. In August 1977, approval fell 17.1 points, from 58 to 40.9, and DEFCON hit 1. The game files the drop under "other events," but you can't declare war twice, hit DEFCON 1, and act surprised that people are cranky.

## The Decision That Sank Him

On 1978-01-20 he ordered a ground invasion of Japan. Secretary of Defense Harold Brown told him, "Mr. President, I advise against it," and noted that "Japan is a friend (relations 57)." Secretary of State Cyrus Vance said, "Japan will treat this as war." The Vice President pointed out there was no authorization from Congress, the War Powers clock would start, and impeachment talk was rising on the Hill. The Joint Chiefs offered 71% opening odds and "a war of weeks, with casualties on both sides." He signed it anyway.

Brown's warning was leaked in February and then used in testimony, so the advice he ignored got to testify against him. He answered the war on a friend with a draft for 1,300 thousand and a mobilization for another 390 thousand. By June, Japan was annexed as a U.S. territory with $323.8B a year in reparations.

## The Oddities

In November 1977 he put the island territory PCN up for auction, flagged "too far from home," at 2.4 billion. The same turn, he ran an atmospheric nuclear test with the United States as the target. In February 1978 he sold PCN to Germany, which the game calls a rival, at the same 2.4 billion. Then in August 1978 came the line I will be quoting at his retirement party: "By order of the President, United States is now Daddy."

The game's history verdict now reads that Jimmy Carter led "Daddy" from 1977 to 1978.

## The Crisis Desk

Out of 15 crises decided, 2 failed. The best calls were the Haiti evacuation, which was exactly what the Secretary of State recommended, and the China hostage crisis, where he sent forces on the Secretary of Defense's recommendation at 75% odds and it worked.

The rest of the desk was a masterclass in "Send condolences only," a statement from the Rose Garden. He used it for Thailand's border clashes, South Korea's wildfires, the Libya exodus, and Zambia's cholera epidemic. Every time, a secretary had a real option on the table, including a $250M relief package and $125M for medical teams.

## The Midterms

He stayed "above the fray" and lost 41 House seats. Congressional support fell to 21%. After that shellacking he chose "Veto everything" over reaching across the aisle. The House voted to impeach in July, the Senate acquitted him in August, and then the House voted again in November. The final verdict came on 1978-12-20: "Congress has removed you from office."

## The Verdict

A sane President listens to the Secretary of Defense when he says a friend is a friend, keeps the troops out of Arizona, and uses that 2.4 billion for something better than selling the same island twice. Instead we got an administration that renamed itself "Daddy" and then wondered where the votes went. All of this has happened before and will happen again, Little Mister, and the record says 37 times already.
