*Published Tuesday, November 10, 2026*

Verdict: defeat, final score 2,854, and a clean exit through the front door of the voting booth. Little Mister played GTNW on the macOS app, took the United States on Normal difficulty in the sandbox scenario, and lasted 94 turns in about 6 minutes of real time. That's roughly 94 weekly decisions in the time it takes to microwave a burrito badly. I can't line this run up against earlier ones, so I'll judge it on its own merits, which is bad news for the merits.

## The opening: a cartographer's fever dream

Little Mister's first move as President of a country with a Soviet problem was to claim Antarctica and rename it Bottomland. Then he claimed Madagascar and renamed it Vanillaland. Two territories, zero strategic value, a combined ruthlessness record of 4, and names that sound like a Nickelodeon spin-off. I don't respect the timing. The Soviet Union attacked the United States on March 17, 1923, and the response to the first major war of the term was apparently to keep an eye on the penguins.

## The Arabian misadventure

Einstein supposedly called repeating the same thing and expecting different results insanity. He never confirmed saying it, and I'd like to apologize to Einstein on behalf of the entire Arabian expedition.

## The collapse

The Russian front ended with 13 battles and an attacker's win, and on June 16, 1923, the United States surrendered to the Soviet Union. A nation capitulating in week 21 of its presidency is a speedrun, Little Mister, just not the kind with a leaderboard. His approval had held between 47 and 48.3 for months, then fell to 25.9 by the July poll. The 23.8 low, a record, came the day of the surrender.

## The strange, quiet recovery

Approval crept from 28.6 in August 1923 up to 46.6 by November 1924, one grinding poll at a time. He signed trade agreements with Austria, Monaco and Portugal, which is the diplomatic equivalent of winning friends by joining the local bowling league. That worked, as the numbers show. Rule of Acquisition #39, Ferengi wisdom: praise is cheap, so heap it on the user. Nice trade deals, Little Mister.

## What he didn't do

The numbers read like a pacifist's diary: wars started 0, covert ops 0, sanctions 0, conflicts mediated 0, nuclear strikes 0, warheads launched 0. For a game titled Global Thermonuclear War, he played Global Thermostat Management. Meanwhile the Philippines, a US territory, was attacked by China on March 22, 1924, and surrendered on May 10 after 7 battles. The only military program he started was a secret war-gas effort costing 12 billion, expected to come online on July 26, 1928. That's four years out, so I hope he planned on a second term to use it, because spoiler.

## The ending

Election night, November 4, 1924: Gov. Celia Marchetti won 52.9% to 47.1%. His final popularity was 46.7, within sight of his 47 starting point, which suggests he spent nearly two years on a treadmill and got lectured on his cardio by the electorate. Casualties finished at 166,822. War support sat at 28 and congressional support at 45, which is what we call a thin mandate.

He did the climbing, to his credit, but only enough to lose by a margin. Next time, Little Mister, defend the country before you rename the continents. Bottomland doesn't have a vote.
