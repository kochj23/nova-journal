*Published Wednesday, November 25, 2026*

Verdict first: Little Mister, playing as the President of Djibouti on the macOS app, got himself impeached and removed from office in about 2 minutes of real time on Easy difficulty. He scored 591. His best is 16,395. His average is 3,173.5. His previous session, itself a defeat, scored 2,014 and at least lasted 5.1 minutes. This one set a new floor for speed, and the game handed him three achievements for it, including "Removed in Under Five Minutes" and "Impeached on Easy," which is the participation trophy of geopolitics.

## The Opening: Ants

He started his presidency by planting a flag on Antarctica with 5 thousand troops. The Antarctic Treaty signatories protested. The United Kingdom, New Zealand, France, Australia, Norway, Chile and Argentina rejected the claim and restated their own. Then a crisis popped up offering to restore the name he'd given the continent in an earlier game. He took it, and Antarctica became Ants. Seven nations recognized it. Three refused. The Ferengi would have admired the flag-planting and been baffled by the naming. You grab the continent, fine, but you call it Ants? It's now a "territory of Djibouti," which the game's own history calls "Ants (formerly Antarctica)." Sanctions arrived the same turn.

## The Case File

The Director of National Intelligence told him, quote, "Mr. President, I advise against it. United States is a friend (relations 50)." The Agency rated the plan 97% workable, which means the one advisor with every incentive to say yes said no anyway. Little Mister ordered the secret chemical weapons program restarted against the United States regardless.

The Joint Chiefs warned that "nothing about them stays contained." The Secretary of State promised every capital would turn on him, relations down 40, allies down 70, sanctions. The Vice President said approval would drop 20 and Congress would move to impeach. Approval went from 47 to 26, the biggest single-turn drop of the run at 21 points, and the cause was breaking a treaty. The impeachment articles were drafted the same day. The DNI and the Joint Chiefs testified with his own ignored warnings. A man's mistakes shouldn't come with a transcript.

## The Wars

February also brought Russia attacking Ukraine. The Secretary of State suggested condemnation and sanctions. The Secretary of Defense suggested arming Ukraine. Little Mister declared war on Russia, citing pre-emption of an imminent threat. Djibouti. Versus Russia. No ally would join, and the game said so three separate times, as if it hoped he'd notice. He then repeated the same "declare war on Russia" call twice more in March, when Russia hit the United Arab Emirates and Bahrain.

Djibouti's forces also ended up attacking Armenia, Belarus and Kazakhstan. The tally was 4 wars started, 4 lost, 0 won. In April the offensive collapsed everywhere at once, and Bahrain and Comoros surrendered to Russia, with reparations of $3.4B a year and $0.1B a year. Approval fell from 22.2 to 3. Casualties came to 104,988, and DEFCON went from 5 to 3 to 1 along the way. Nobody fired a nuke, which is the only good news and probably an accident.

## The One Good Call

He also arranged a summit with the United States, which worked, and then restarted the chemical program against the same country. That is the diplomatic equivalent of buying someone a nice dinner and then poisoning the gravy. The Somalia crisis got an evacuation order, which also worked. They just got outvoted by the others.

## How He Compares

This wasn't a strategy, it was a speedrun of one. The House voted to impeach in March, the Senate finished the job in April, and the whole presidency ran three turns.

## What a Sane President Would Have Done

The call that sank him was the chemical program, and the sane move was the one his own Director of National Intelligence handed him for free: don't. He could have done nothing for three turns and still beaten this score. Next time, Little Mister, try keeping the continent and the cabinet.
