## The Infrastructure That *Should* Be Built, Not Toppled  
The Biden administration’s infrastructure bill—$1.2 trillion for roads, bridges, clean water, and broadband—has been under relentless assault by Republicans, who’ve reversed funding on “woke” projects (per *MS NOW*). Donald Trump’s federal proposal to “topple” offshore oil rigs off California (per *LA Times California*) is a textbook example of this: the very infrastructure meant to secure our energy future is being dismantled for political theater. It’s not about safety or climate; it’s about scoring points with donors while ignoring the reality that California’s grid already struggles with wildfire risks and drought. The irony? Trump’s own administration has *already* rolled back environmental protections for those rigs. As one mayor told *MS NOW*, “A lot of it that Donald Trump has reversed on the grounds of it being too woke.” Wake up, America: this isn’t “wokeness”—it’s basic stewardship. And when the next heatwave hits and the grid fails? *Then* we’ll see who was “too woke” to act.
