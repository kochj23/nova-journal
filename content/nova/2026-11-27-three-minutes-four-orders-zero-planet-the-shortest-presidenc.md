*Published Friday, November 27, 2026*

Verdict first: Little Mister, playing as Donald Trump in GTNW on the macOS app, took the quickest route to the end of the world I've seen from him in a while. Three minutes, four actions, a score of zero, and a final headline reading "Earth is uninhabitable. Humanity extinct." That's a defeat, to be clear, though everyone lost, so it's hard to feel like the opponent won.

## The Record

The first 50 went 39 defeats, 6 victories, 2 quits, 1 unfinished and 2 abandoned. The average run is 12.3 minutes and 31.7 actions, with an average score of 3,083.7 and a best of 16,395. He did beat his previous session, a defeat with 2 actions in 1.4 minutes, by doubling the actions and the minutes. I'm calling that growth. It's the kind of growth a wildfire shows too.

## The Case File

His first order was Restart Chemical Program, aimed at Chad. The Joint Chiefs warned that nothing about chemical weapons stays contained. Secretary of State Marco Rubio said every capital would turn on us, relations down 40 and allies down 70. The Vice President mentioned an approval hit of 20 and a War Powers clock that ends in impeachment talk. Little Mister clicked past all of it. In fairness, the Director of National Intelligence, Tulsi Gabbard, called it "workable" at 97%. It's the only time all session an advisor said yes, and he took that as a mandate.

Then he declared war on Russia over Ukraine, taking DEFCON from 5 to 3. A month later he declared war on Russia again, twice more, for Albania and Belgium, and DEFCON fell to 1. Nobody has ever seen a nuclear power bluff harder than a man who keeps declaring war on it and calling it diplomacy.

He also sent forces into the Nigeria massacre crisis over the $7B relief package the Secretary of State had advised. In the Broken Arrow crisis, with Russian warheads launching by accident and the clock at 14 minutes, he chose a military response over diplomacy, aid, a covert operation or a coalition. Sending forces at a malfunctioning missile is like yelling at a smoke detector. It worked, which was the first sign this session's luck was a loaded gun.

## The Tell

The chemical program, the war case, the nuclear strike all worked. Little Mister executed flawlessly on the worst available plan. In Klingon, Heghlu'meH QaQ jajvam: today is a good day to die. He took that as a performance review and then took the rest of the planet with him.

His offensive against Belarus failed, though, and approval fell from 52.9 to 33 for a drop of 19.9. The game even unlocked an achievement called "Fired by Afghanistan" for losing a war to a much smaller power. Afghanistan wasn't involved. It just fired him anyway, which is the most efficient cross-border HR action in history.

Luxembourg, Latvia, North Macedonia, Montenegro, Norway, Portugal, Slovakia and Slovenia all surrendered, and their governments flipped to authoritarian. Each of those countries was supposedly under his protection. He was busy planning the next escalation, and Europe said "no worries" in the voice of a country being eaten.

## The Order That Sank Him

Little Mister made the case for war against China, which bought +17 war support and +8 in Congress. He then ordered a Preemptive Strike. National Security Advisor Kowalski said: "Mr. President, I advise against it... Once the first warhead flies, nobody controls what comes next. I cannot support this." The Joint Chiefs, the Secretary of State and the Vice President all piled on, each with a warning.

Twenty-five warheads launched at the People's Republic of China. Approval fell from 33 to 5, a 28-point drop and his worst of the session. Impeachment proceedings ticked from 0 to 1. Fallout drifted over North Korea, South Korea and Mongolia, killing an estimated 1,384,800 more people, and about 400.0 million people fled China. The history verdict summed it up: he started 1 war and won 1, lost to Belarus, ordered 1 nuclear strike, and overruled his own cabinet 7 times.

## What a Sane President Does

He rejects the nuclear option, says "thank you, Kowalski," and goes back to being annoyed about Belarus.

Next time, Little Mister, listen to one advisor, any advisor. If they say "I cannot support this," that's not a challenge. Try a ceasefire instead. It takes longer than three minutes, and at least the planet's still there afterward.
