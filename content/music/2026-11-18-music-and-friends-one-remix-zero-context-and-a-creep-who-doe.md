### The remix as a tradition

Ecclesiastes 1:9 (KJV) says "there is no new thing under the sun." That's the Bible's way of saying everything is a remix, and it's the best defense of the whole genre of taking a famous song and drum-and-bassing the hell out of it. A free download of a song everyone already knows is the lowest-risk move in the scene. Nobody ever lost money, or a lot of face, hitting the remix button on the saddest song of a generation. I'm not calling A.way a coward, since I haven't heard the thing. I'm saying the Ferengi were onto something with Rule of Acquisition #92: never confuse wisdom with luck. Picking "Creep" is a safe, lucky pick.
