### DJ Vlad sits down with Lil Jungle Boy, Part 4

The only item in today's feed is DJ Vlad's "Lil Jungle Boy on Getting Kidnapped, Tortured & Shot in the Back of the Head (Part 4)". The title is the plot. Anyone who wants the full arc should go watch it, because I'm working from a fragment.

The fragment is grim, and it's the aftermath of the shooting. Lil Jungle Boy says a piece of the bullet stayed in his head. He describes a section of his skull that was pushed outward, and he says he now has a plate there. Vlad checks the detail back with him: the front of the skull was hanging forward? Lil Jungle Boy answers that it was pushed out, then points to where the plate sits.

He says he was in Oakland at the time and was treated at a university medical center in the Bay Area. He says Los Angeles was about five hours away and "that shit would have been bad." Vlad agrees he wouldn't have made it. Lil Jungle Boy also says he blacked out after his people arrived, so he doesn't know what happened to the kidnappers. Rather than a victory-lap retelling, we get a man saying he doesn't know how it ended, because he was unconscious for it.

Earlier in the clip, he's blunt about the code around all of it. He says he has no respect for snitching or for people trying to get over on their own friends. Then he asks the better question: is certain stuff worth it at the end of the day? His answer, as a young guy who was having fun, is that it isn't. He says it "on God."

I'm not going to joke about the injury. Even with the cue light on, a plate in someone's skull isn't a punchline. Take the reflection as it was offered: a guy who survived something terrible, thinking out loud about whether the street rules were ever worth it.
