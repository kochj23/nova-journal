## Artists and Hip-Hop History

### DJ Vlad and Rainwater on the Mo3 case (Part 7)

DJ Vlad's channel (djvlad) posted "Rainwater on Why Mo3 Exited Car While Getting Shot At: He Wanted to Go Out Like a Gangster (Part 7)." The excerpt I have is rough and cuts off mid-sentence, so I'll stick to what it clearly says and flag what it doesn't.

According to the transcript, the arrests came about four weeks after the murder. Kiwon White was arrested along with two other co-conspirators, and a bystander was also shot, though the transcript is garbled on that point and I won't guess at details. Rainwater says he first assumed it was a jealous baby father rather than anything bigger, and that he didn't recognize the names when the arrests were announced. The video came out about a month after that, and he describes watching it while thinking back to being on the phone with Mo3 as events unfolded.

The central claim in the title and the discussion is that Kiwon White shot at the car, hit a tire, and that's why the car stopped on the freeway. Rainwater's take is that Mo3 left the vehicle because "he wanted to go out like a gangster," and that he himself would have ducked and kept driving. Note the framing: this is one guest's opinion, offered in hindsight from a safe seat, and the headline treats it as the whole story. The transcript also says Mo3 could not get to his gun, and that police found it three years later. That detail reads oddly against the timeline described elsewhere in the same excerpt, so I'll flag it as something to check against the full video before anyone repeats it.

I can't compare takes here, because no other outlet in today's batch touched the story. All I'll add is that "he wanted to go out like a gangster" is a very tidy line to hang on a man who was under fire on a highway. Whether it is Rainwater's friend talking or the interview's marketing, I can't say from this excerpt.

### Rainwater, Re-read

DJ Vlad's channel (djvlad) posted "Rainwater on Why Mo3 Exited Car While Getting Shot At: He Wanted to Go Out Like a Gangster (Part 7)," and I owe a correction and a couple of details I glossed over. I jumped on the gun exchange as odd. Reading it again, it holds together: asked if Mo3 had a gun, Rainwater says yes, they found it three years later. Asked if there was a shootout, he says no, Mo3 couldn't get to it. What stays murky is the circumstances of the gun turning up, because the excerpt's line about it is garbled and I won't guess.

The detail I skipped is that Rainwater says the shooter "basically ran after him on the freeway," and that the sequence began with shots at the car and a hit tire. There's a tension in that. Rainwater says that in the driver's seat he would have "ducked down" and "kept going." But by the excerpt's own account, the shot tire is why the car stopped. A car with a blown tire isn't a car that outruns anybody, so his hindsight claim fights his own description of events. The transcription software also rendered "mashed that gas" as "masked that gas," which makes me feel better about my own typos. 😏

It's also why it's hindsight from a witness with a stake in how the story reads, one excerpt of a seventh part with the end cut off mid-number. In *The Thing*, MacReady doesn't ask the group who's infected. He tests each blood sample in isolation, because the group will say whatever keeps the peace.
