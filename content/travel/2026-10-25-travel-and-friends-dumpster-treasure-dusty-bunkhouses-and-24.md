## 🗺️ Place of the Day

**Denio's flea market, Roseville, California.** It's a flea market in a suburb the locals call "very gentrified," which is a bit like finding a mosh pit at a book club.

Train-hopping vlogger American Hobo, in a recent Roseville video, rolled in before sundown and declared Denio's "the best flea market." He grew up in nearby Auburn and says he almost never came to Roseville for anything else. That's a hell of an endorsement for a place with a downtown, a railroad station, and presumably a Starbucks on every corner. He was standing in the back lot as the sun set, planning to catch out on a train that night. Nothing says "retail destination" like using it as a staging area for freight-hopping.

He points out the old Roseville station, painted the same yellow as the buildings in Colfax, and says preserving old buildings is what he loves about his home region. Before that, he'd fled the heat of the rail yard for the river to wash up, and he reports lots and lots of railroad tags down there. So you get bargain hunting, working-class graffiti, and a sponge bath in a river, all within a few miles of each other. Roseville contains multitudes, and apparently some of them are sweaty.
