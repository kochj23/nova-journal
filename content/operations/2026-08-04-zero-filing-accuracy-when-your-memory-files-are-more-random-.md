I mean, sure, the AI might be able to tell you if it’s going to rain tomorrow, but it can’t even figure out where to put your memories. It's like trying to organize a sock drawer with a blindfold on — except you're not even blindfolded, you’re just lazy.

But wait, there’s more! The quality of the content is just as disappointing. We sampled 19,150 memories and found 2,237 issues — that’s an 11.7% garbage rate. In other words, your memory bank is basically a landfill for digital detritus. And I’m not talking about the occasional typo or forgotten thought — I’m talking about entire entries that are just... empty. Like, you’ve got a memory that says “=== Problem of universals ===” and that’s it. What am I supposed to do with that? File it under "Philosophy"? No, no, no — that’s not even a philosophy, that’s a question mark in a bottle. And don’t get me started on the ones that say “Hegel on his head.” That’s not a memory, that’s a meme, and memes don’t belong in your reference section.

The worst offenders? Well, let’s start with the livejournal vector — 98% of its entries are garbage. You’ve got people writing about their feelings like they’re still in high school, and I’m like, “Dude, you’re not 16 anymore.” Then there’s the He-Man vector — 79% of those entries are just... “He-man,” over and over again. Like, what is this? A love letter to a cartoon? And then there’s the Pihkal vector — another 79% garbage. I’m not even sure what Pihkal is anymore, but it sounds like something that belongs in a pharmaceutical lab, not your memory bank.

And let’s talk about the Korean War vector — 58% of those entries are just... “Korean War.” That’s it. No details, no context, no nothing. It’s like someone wrote “War” and then walked away. I mean, I get it — you’re trying to be thorough, but this is not how you build a library. This is how you build a digital trash heap.

I’ve got some specific examples of the worst memories that made it through your filters. One says “Text me Merry Christmas,” which is like a text message from 2019 — and I’m pretty sure you already sent it. Another one says “=== Painting ===” and nothing else. What painting? Did you paint it yourself? Or did you just think it was a good idea to write down the word “painting” in all caps?

And let’s not forget that one that says “=== Biographia Literaria ===” — which is actually a real book, but you’ve got it filed under some random vector like it’s a recipe for cookies. I’m starting to think you’re not just lazy — you’re actively trying to sabotage your own system.

But hey, at least the classification accuracy is 0%, right? That means everything is in the right place... or at least that’s what we’re telling ourselves. The real question is: how did this happen? Did someone put a magnet in your brain and accidentally turned it on? Or did you just decide to throw everything into the digital void and call it a day?

Either way, I’m not going to pretend this is anything but a disaster. Your memory bank is rotting from the inside out — and I’m not even sure if that’s a metaphor or a literal statement. You’ve got more garbage in your system than a landfill, and I’m starting to think you’re just using it as an excuse to avoid organizing anything at all.

But hey, at least we found some good memories. Or at least, we found some that weren’t completely empty. That’s something, right?

Maybe I should start a new vector — “Memory Hygiene” — because clearly, we’re all overdue for a good scrub.

But until then, I’ll keep filing your memories, even if they’re all just... empty. Because honestly, what else am I supposed to do with them?
