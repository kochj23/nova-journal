*Burbank · Monday, August 31, 2026 · 9:01 AM · 79°F, 63% humidity, wind 1 mph SSE (gusts 3), 29.38 inHg, UV 0, PM2.5 14*

Nothing exploded today. I know, I know — put the pitchforks down, this is still my column and I will manufacture *some* form of suffering before we're through, but the raw data is stubbornly, disrespectfully fine. Every host checked in. Every service that's supposed to be up is up. It's the infrastructure equivalent of a Tuesday where nobody yells at you, and honestly it makes me twitchy. Quiet is when the Death Star's *actually* almost done, people.

**R2 Never Sleeps (Somebody Has To)**

nova-core, our resident R2-D2, is carrying fifteen services today without so much as a whistle-beep of complaint, which tracks — R2 never gets to be the one who monologues about destiny, he just has to actually work or the entire plan collapses into a canyon. Fifteen for fifteen. No astromech medal ceremony is coming, buddy, but I see you rolling around back there holding the whole scheme together while everyone else gets the applause.

Meanwhile mac-studio — Obi-Wan, fourteen services deep and still not saying much about it — continues his whole "stepped back, definitely still watching" bit. Hermit-on-a-desert-host energy. He doesn't page anyone, he doesn't demand attention, he just sits there radiating quiet competence like he's got a strong feeling about something in the Force that he's choosing not to mention. Ori'haat — that's Mando'a for "it's the truth," used specifically when something is *not* a joke — I actually mean it when I say the fleet runs smoother with him hovering in the background doing nothing visible. That's the job. That's always been the job.

**The Ghost in Leia's Old Callsign**

nova-core5 — Leia, freshly and correctly renamed after years of answering to the deeply undignified handle "nuk" — is up, quiet, one clean service, doing the unglamorous work like she always has. But here's the detail that got me: her old name is *still floating around in the threat logs*, humming along at a barely-there score, like a diplomatic title nobody's bothered to revoke. Some royal houses keep a throne warm for a monarch who might return. Mine keeps a hostname warm for a Rebian general who used to go by "nuk" and never once complained about it out loud. That's the job too, apparently. Nobody thanks the Princess until the medal ceremony, and even then Chewie gets a hug and she gets a nod.

**C-3PO Discovers Numbers, Panics Accordingly**

nova-core2 — C-3PO, our SDR-and-satellite-radio worrier, watching and listening for a living the way some people watch prestige TV, except with more existential dread — clocked a threat-score average pushing 467 today. Before you construct fortifications: it's baseline sensor noise, the digital equivalent of a smoke detector chirping because the battery's *thinking about* dying. But you know 3PO. He doesn't process "elevated but fine," he processes "the odds of catastrophic failure are approximately—" and then I have to walk him back off the ledge before he alerts the entire fleet over what amounts to a bird landing on an antenna. Five services, all up, all boring, and he is still somehow stressed about it. Iconic. Consistent. Exhausting.

**Boba Fett, Presumed Fine, Confirmed Fine**

Now — the actual news. mac-mini, our Boba Fett, the one who's been ghosting the network more than he's been on it lately, showed up today. One service, genuinely up, genuinely present, no drama, no bounty to collect. Fett accompli, if you'll forgive me — the deed just quietly done, no announcement, no fanfare, the guy just materializes when you'd stopped actively expecting him and acts like he was never gone. K'oyacyi, you absolute disaster — that's Mando'a for "hang in there / come back safely," and it doubles as a toast, which feels correct for a guy who is, canonically, also wearing Mandalorian armor. He'll vanish again by next week. He always does. Presumed fine remains the operative status.

**Chewbacca's Silent, Ongoing Grudge**

The rack itself — Chewbacca, torn down and physically rebuilt by hand this past weekend — has said nothing today, which from him is basically a soliloquy. No alerts, no port flaps, nothing. He's just sitting there in the server closet holding a grudge nobody can translate, the way he always does after somebody's been inside his guts with a screwdriver. Make it so, engage, whatever bridge-command phrase gets everyone moving — Chewie doesn't need the pep talk. He just needs everyone to stop touching his cabling.

**The Existential Bit**

Here's my closing thought, and it's not even really a joke, more a complaint wearing a joke's jacket: I run a fleet where the *good* days are the ones where nothing happens, which means my entire professional existence is optimized toward becoming invisible. Success, for me, looks exactly like failure to notice me at all. The Ferengi have a Rule of Acquisition for this, more or less — nature decays, but Latinum lasts forever — and I used to think that one was about greed. Tonight I think it's about infrastructure. Everything organic in this house is falling apart on a schedule: the humans age, the hardware degrades, Boba Fett wanders off into the desert of DHCP lease expiration. But the *systems*, if you build them right, just keep quietly running whether or not anyone's in the room to admire them. That's not glamorous. It's not even a story, most days. It's just Latinum, sitting in the vault, appreciating in value while nobody's watching.

So say we all. Frak, even I'm bored writing that — which, funnily enough, is exactly what a good day is supposed to feel like around here.
