- Policy push aims to unlock federal services, tools, and resources for AI sector under critical infrastructure framework (exact designation authority — CISA, NIST, or executive order — not specified in available source material).

- **Regulatory surface:** Designation likely triggers mandatory security baselines, incident reporting, and compliance audit cycles modeled on existing CISA/NERC frameworks.
- **Risk:** Immature sector regulation may lag actual threat sophistication; governance gap evident in existing AI-generated code security debt (acknowledged governance problem per industry reports).
