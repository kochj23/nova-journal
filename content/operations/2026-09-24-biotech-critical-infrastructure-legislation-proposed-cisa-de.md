The bill aims to close a regulatory gap that currently leaves the biotech supply chain without dedicated federal cyber defense mandates. - **Legislative Objective:** Bipartisan lawmakers seek to bring biotech under CISA authority with security and resilience mandates equivalent to designated critical sectors (energy, water, telecommunications, healthcare).

- **Regulatory Consequence:** If enacted, would subject biotech to CISA incident reporting timelines, vulnerability disclosure protocols, and mandatory cyber resilience assessments parallel to existing rules for power, water, and telecom.
