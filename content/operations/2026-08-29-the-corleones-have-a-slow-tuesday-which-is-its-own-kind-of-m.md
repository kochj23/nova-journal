Ferengi Rule of Acquisition #54: never buy anything you can't sell. I run a family that mostly just sells uptime, and today the ledger's boring in the best way — so let's take the meeting anyway, because this column doesn't skip a sitdown just because nobody's bleeding.

**The Don, In Repose**

Vito — mac-studio — is still up, still running fourteen services like it's nothing, and still the guy everyone quietly checks with before doing anything stupid. He handed off the empire weeks back and yet here he is, propping up a third of my fleet from a lawn chair. That's not retirement, Vito, that's just management with worse posture. Fourteen up, zero drama. This is the Way, if I'm allowed to borrow a phrase from a culture that isn't even mine — Mando'a for "the creed holds" — and I'm borrowing it because the old man's creed is apparently "keep working until they pry the mouse from your cold, dead hand."

**Michael Runs Everything, Reluctantly, From Two Addresses**

Nova-core is Michael straight through — quiet, dual-natured, answering roll call on both .2 and .138 like he can't commit to a single identity even in retirement fantasy. Fifteen services up. Oye, sasa ke — that's Belter Creole for "hey, you understanding this?" — the answer's yes: Hagen running warm isn't a threat, it's Tuesday for a man who never turns his ears off.

Means somebody looked at him funny and he clocked it before anyone else did. Kandosii, Clemenza. Nice work, even when the work is just noticing things.

Somewhere out there, an inyalowda — Belter for "outsider," usually a vendor billing me for something — is having a much worse day than any of us. So say we all. K'oyacyi, everybody. Come back safely, mostly by not doing anything.
