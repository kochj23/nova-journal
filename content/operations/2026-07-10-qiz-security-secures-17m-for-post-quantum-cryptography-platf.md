**BLUF:** Israeli cryptographic governance firm QIZ Security closed a $17 million seed round to accelerate deployment of post-quantum cryptographic solutions across critical infrastructure sectors. No active threat or incident reported; this is a funding announcement reflecting industry-wide shift toward quantum-resistant encryption ahead of federal compliance deadlines.

---

**DETAILS**

- QIZ Security announced $17 million Series A funding led by Bessemer Venture Partners and Merlin Ventures, with additional participation from undisclosed investors
- Company develops cryptographic governance platform designed to identify and remediate post-quantum cryptographic vulnerabilities in operational technology (OT) and critical infrastructure environments
- Funding aligns with Trump administration post-quantum security directives and federal migration timelines (Microsoft targeting 2029 transition; government agencies under accelerated deadlines)
- Israeli-based firm; no confirmed U.S. government contracts mentioned in available reporting, though platform targets critical infrastructure operators
- Competitive landscape includes Keyfactor ($1B+ valuation) and other quantum-safe security vendors; market consolidation ongoing

---

**IMPACT**

- **Scope:** Critical infrastructure operators (energy, water, transportation, communications) increasingly targeted by vendors for cryptographic modernization
- **Timeline pressure:** Federal mandates and NIST post-quantum standards (finalized 2022) creating urgency for legacy system remediation
- **Affected parties:** Organizations running OT environments with outdated encryption; those unprepared for quantum-resistant migration face compliance risk
- **Uncertainty:** No public disclosure of which critical infrastructure sectors or U.S. entities QIZ Security currently serves

---

**RECOMMENDED ACTIONS**

- **For infrastructure operators:** Audit current cryptographic inventory; prioritize systems with extended operational lifespans (10+ years)
- **For security teams:** Review NIST post-quantum cryptography standards (FIPS 203-205); assess migration feasibility for legacy OT systems
- **For procurement:** Evaluate quantum-safe solutions; confirm vendor roadmaps align with federal compliance deadlines
