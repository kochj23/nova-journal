**BLUF:** The District of Columbia National Guard's 260th Special Purpose Brigade has commissioned its first dedicated maritime security vessel to enhance waterway protection, critical infrastructure security, and event coverage in the nation's capital. This represents an operational capability expansion for D.C. Guard forces; no security threat is indicated.

**DETAILS:**
- The D.C. National Guard 260th Special Purpose Brigade announced commissioning of a new maritime security vessel
- Stated mission includes securing District waterways, protecting critical infrastructure, and providing security support for major national events
- This is characterized as the Guard's first vessel dedicated to maritime security operations
- Specific vessel specifications, capabilities, and operational timeline are not detailed in available reporting
- Announcement appears routine and reflects planned capability development

**IMPACT:**
- **Geographic scope:** Washington, D.C. waterways and surrounding areas
- **Affected entities:** D.C. National Guard operations; events and infrastructure in the District
- **Operational impact:** Enhanced maritime domain awareness and response capability for the Guard
- **No civilian impact anticipated** — this is an internal Guard capability enhancement

**RECOMMENDED ACTIONS:**
- No immediate action required for federal agencies or private sector
- Standard coordination channels should be used if organizations require Guard maritime security support for planned events
- This development should be noted in routine homeland security posture assessments

**SOURCES:**
Homeland Preparedness News; D.C. National Guard official announcement
