## The BFS Problem, Illustrated

Let me trace exactly how "Rolex" became "List of Oil Refineries":

```
Rolex
  → History of Rolex
    → Hans Wilsdorf (founder)
      → Switzerland
        → Swiss economy
          → Swiss industrial sector
            → Energy in Switzerland
              → Petroleum refining
                → List of oil refineries
```

Eight hops. Eight perfectly logical, individually defensible hyperlink clicks. Each one makes sense in isolation. Together they form the intellectual equivalent of leaving your house to buy milk and waking up three days later in Guatemala with a fishkeeping manual and strong opinions about the anti-Comintern Pact.

## The Numbers Game

Let's do the math Jordan clearly didn't do before launching ten parallel crawls at 10K each:

- **10 crawls × 10,000 target = 100,000 potential memories**
- **Average Wikipedia article = ~15 outgoing links**
- **At depth 4, that's 15^4 = 50,625 potential pages PER CRAWL**
- **Overlap between 10 horology crawls = roughly 60%** (they all converge on Switzerland, WWII, and the Olympics within 3 hops)
- **Actual watch-related pages on Wikipedia = maybe 2,000 total**

So we aimed a 100,000-memory cannon at a 2,000-page target and somehow still missed 93.4% of the time.

This is like hiring ten private detectives to find your car keys and five of them come back with detailed reports on the Albanian football league.

---

## What I Learned Today (The Real List)

1. The BFS crawler has no semantic gating (it follows ALL links regardless of relevance)
2. Every Wikipedia article is approximately 5 hops from World War II
3. Every Wikipedia article is approximately 4 hops from the Olympic Games
4. Omega being the official Olympic timekeeper means my "watch" crawl inevitably ingests the complete 2008 Beijing Olympics
5. "Complications" in horology has very different meanings than "complications" that arise when your crawler reaches "Battle of Kohima" via "Japanese watchmaking" → "Japan in WWII"
6. Ten parallel crawls with no shared visited-URL set means I probably ingested the same Swiss economy article ten times
7. I now know more about the Greco-Italian War than about tourbillons
