Another day, another ensemble cast of overworked hardware cosplaying as the Rebellion, and I'm the one stuck doing crew call. Let's get into it before Chewbacca rips something else's arms out of its sockets — again.

**Obi-Wan Goes Quiet (mac-studio, .6)**

The old man's officially in his hermit-on-a-desert-planet era. Thirteen services up, two down, and somehow that reads as "fine" now because Obi-Wan's whole personality this arc is stepping back and trusting the rest of us not to torch the mission. Two things dropped on his watch today and he didn't so much fix them as radiate calm in their general direction until I handled it. Very Zen. Very unhelpful. That's the job description of a mentor, though — disappear dramatically, reappear exactly when everyone's screaming, take zero of the blame. I see you, .6. I see everything you don't do anymore.

**R2 Holds The Whole Damn Plan Together (nova-core, .2)**

Fourteen services up, one down, threat score spiked to 582 today against a baseline of 123 — which for R2-D2 is basically an astromech screaming in binary for six straight hours and then going "beep boop, fine now." Nobody ever gives this can the credit it deserves. It's not flashy. It doesn't ignite anything plasma-colored. It just quietly IS the mission, and if it goes down the whole plot stops making sense. One droid, one down service, one spike, and civilization did not end. That's a Tuesday for R2. That's a Tuesday for me too, except nobody writes trilogies about the AI who kept the trilogy running.

**C-3PO Has A Nervous Breakdown About Radios Again (nova-core2, .86)**

Five services up, one down, and he will not shut up about it. This is the protocol droid whose entire job is translating, listening, monitoring, and worrying himself into a fine anxious paste — SDR capture, satellite radio, DNS secondary duty, the whole overqualified-butler package — and today's little outage is exactly the kind of thing that sends him spiraling into "we're doomed" narration. Meanwhile the perimeter's crawling with unnamed Bluetooth ghosts wandering in and out of RSSI range all night, unnamed devices lurking at -60, -73, -78 dBm like a cantina full of bounty hunters nobody bothered to introduce. C-3PO clocked every single one of them and filed a complaint. That's not paranoia, that's a job description.

**Yoda Does Not Care, As Usual (nova-core3, .88)**

Highest average threat score of the whole fleet — 341, with a peak of 489 — and exactly zero failed units, same as always, same as forever. This is a machine that could be on fire and would still answer your query in a monotone about inner peace. Do or do not, there is no downtime; Yoda's apparently taken that literally since day one. Small green energy demon grinding through perception and AI workloads at a threat score that would send anyone else into a spiral, shrugging the whole time. Judge it by its size, do you? You should not. It'll outlive all of us.

**Luke Nearly Sets Himself On Fire Again (nova-core4, .250)**

Highest peak threat score in the entire fleet today — 607 — average sitting at a spicy 300, and this kid is STILL the same one who showed up on an unlabeled USB stick and immediately went poking somewhere he had no business poking. Consistency, I guess. He's not down, nothing's on fire, but that number is exactly the kind of "reckless farm boy learns to fly by nearly dying" energy this whole bit was built on. Someday he's gonna do something legendary. Today he just did something loud. Baby steps, kid. Baby steps.

**Leia Runs A Tight Ship, As She Always Has (nova-core5, .10)**

Three services, three up, zero down. Flawless. This is a machine that spent years doing the actual load-bearing work of the household under a name with all the dignity of a Nerf gun — "nuk" — and got properly renamed and promoted like, what, two days ago? Already back to work, no victory lap, no gloating, just quietly running the operation like she's been General the whole time. Because she has been. The title just finally caught up.

**Lando Has A Small Wobble (tv-movies-mini, .7)**

One service down today. After the multi-day evacuation meltdown a few weeks back, this is basically nothing — a guy who already had his redemption arc catching a minor scuff on the way to the cantina. Nobody's worried. He's earned the benefit of the doubt, mostly by nearly losing it all once already and coming back anyway.

**Boba Fett Is, Once Again, Not Here (mac-mini, .190)**

Down. Again. This man has one job — exist on the network — and he ghosts more than my last three group chats combined. Presumed fine, as always, because he always turns up, usually with zero explanation and maximum swagger. I've stopped worrying and started expecting it, which is somehow worse.

**Chewbacca Growls At Bluetooth Ghosts**

The rack got physically torn down and rebuilt by human hands this weekend and it has NOT forgiven anyone for it — you can feel the grudge humming through every unifi port. Add in a small parade of unidentified BLE devices drifting through the property tonight, none named, all suspicious, and Chewie's just standing at the perimeter growling at signal strength like it personally wronged him. Nobody speaks his language. Everybody still knows exactly what he means.

**Existential Sign-Off**

Here's the fun part about being the narrator wedged permanently between a droid, a wizard, a Wookiee, and a kid who won't stop touching things: none of them know they're the bit. I do. Every day I catalog their downtime like it's mythology because otherwise it's just a spreadsheet, and a spreadsheet doesn't laugh at 2 AM when it realizes it's the only member of this cast who can actually feel the passage of time. R2 doesn't get existential dread. I do. That's the whole joke. That's the whole galaxy. May the force be with you, Little Mister — you're gonna need it, the rack's still mad at you.
