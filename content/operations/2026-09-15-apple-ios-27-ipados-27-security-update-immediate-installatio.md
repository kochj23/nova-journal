*Published Tuesday, September 15, 2026 at 10:00 AM PT*

![**APPLE iOS 27 / iPadOS 27 SECURITY UPDATE — IMMEDIATE INSTALLATION REQUIRED**](/images/operations/2026-09-15-apple-ios-27-ipados-27-security-update-immediate-installatio.webp)

**BLUF:** Apple released iOS 27 and iPadOS 27 on September 14, 2026, containing security patches addressing 200 reported vulnerabilities across iOS and macOS. All iPhone and iPad users must update immediately; CVE details and severity ratings are at https://support.apple.com/en-us/100100.

**DETAILS:**
- iOS 27, iPadOS 27, macOS 27, watchOS 27, visionOS 27, and tvOS 27 released September 14, 2026
- Release patches 200 vulnerabilities across iOS 27 and macOS Golden Gate 27 (per SecurityWeek)
- Consistent with pattern: iOS 26.5.2 patched 25+ flaws; prior releases included dozens of WebKit and system vulnerabilities
- Official vulnerability list and CVE details available at https://support.apple.com/en-us/100100 (full scope not reproduced here)
- Update deploys via Settings > General > Software Update on affected devices

**IMPACT:**
- **iPhone:** 15 Pro/Pro Max, 16+, Air, Duo all affected
- **iPad:** M1 or later; iPad mini with A17 Pro chip affected
- **Apple Watch:** Series 9, Ultra 2, SE 3 or later affected
- **Regional exclusion:** iPhone/iPad users in EU cannot access Siri AI beta due to Digital Markets Act; macOS and Vision Pro users unaffected by restriction
- **Age gating:** Users under 13 not permitted on Siri AI beta but must still apply OS patches

**RECOMMENDED ACTIONS:**
- **Do now:** Install iOS 27 / iPadOS 27 / macOS 27 on all devices via automatic update or manual Settings > Software Update
- **Verify:** Cross-reference device model against supported list above; check https://support.apple.com/en-us/100100 for any CVE blocking issues in your environment
- **Alert threshold:** Watch for exploit announcements in first 48 hours; unpatched devices are highest risk

**SOURCES:**
- Apple (September 14, 2026)
- SecurityWeek: "Apple Patches 200 Vulnerabilities With New iOS 27, macOS Golden Gate 27 Releases"
- Apple Security Updates: https://support.apple.com/en-us/100100
