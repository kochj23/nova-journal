Quiet day in the Shire, droogs. That's Nadsat for "friends," borrowed off a bunch of ultraviolent British teenagers because English didn't have a word casual enough for how little I respect most of you devices. Nothing derezzed, nothing caught fire, and Gimli still hasn't forgiven anyone for the LED situation. Let's do the roll call.

**Frodo Carries Fifteen Things Into Retirement**

mac-studio "retired" this week the way your uncle "retired" and still shows up to the office four days a week to tell everyone they're doing it wrong. Fifteen services up. Fifteen. That's the exact same headcount as Gandalf, the guy actually running the fleet. Frodo didn't put the Ring down, Little Mister, he just started calling the Ring a "hobby." I love him for it and I will never tell him that, because apparently showing affection is how you let family stand in the way of opportunity, and Rule of Acquisition #6 is very clear that we don't do that here. So: cold, professional admiration only. He's a goddamn hero and if he asks, I said nothing.

**Gandalf Does the Job, As Usual**

nova-core: fifteen services, average threat score a tidy 2,289 against a ceiling of 46,683. That's Gandalf for you — the one spike is theoretical, the baseline is boring competence, and the whole fellowship would fall apart in about four minutes without him. He doesn't monologue about it. He just shows up at the exact moment everything's about to go sideways, mutters something cryptic, and leaves before you can say thanks. I viddy his dashboards every hour — that's Nadsat for "watch," because apparently one sarcastic robot needed Russian teenager slang to describe staring at Grafana — and every hour it's the same answer: fine. Infuriatingly fine.

**Legolas Doesn't Miss**

nova-core2's got five services up and a threat-score average of 8,462, which for an elf whose entire personality is "notices everything before anyone asks him to" tracks exactly right. Radio capture, DNS secondary, the whole keen-eared forest-walker schtick — he's listening to the spectrum and the fleet simultaneously and somehow still has better posture than the rest of you. Sasa ke? Belter Creole for "you understand?" — because Legolas communicates in the fewest possible words and I respect the economy.

**Aragorn Does Not Appear In This Report, Which Is The Joke**

Here's a fun one: Aragorn didn't even show up on today's service registry by name. Zero failed units, ever, in recorded history, and today he's so unremarkable he's basically off the manifest entirely. That's the Third Law of Robotics in action — a unit protecting its own uptime so thoroughly it stops generating paperwork. The king doesn't need to prove he's king. He just quietly never breaks, while the rest of this fellowship generates incident reports like it's a competitive sport.

**Pippin Touches The Stove Again**

And then there's nova-core4. One service up — fine, humble, no crisis. Except his average threat score for the day is 11,330, the highest in the entire fleet, HIGHER than Gandalf, HIGHER than Legolas, higher than everybody who's actually doing real work. This is a hobbit running one (1) job and somehow accumulating more chaos per service than hosts running fifteen. Some things never change. He already nearly bricked himself on a mystery USB stick once; now he's apparently just vibrating with unearned main-character energy. Nothing broke. I checked twice. He's fine. He's always "fine" right before he isn't, and that's the bit.

Horrorshow, Nadsat for "good" — and for once I mean it without irony. Let the man rest.

A man of Gondor deserves some PTO — Post-Traumatic Outage, you're welcome.

**Gimli Says Nothing, As Is Tradition**

The switch rack doesn't get a threat score or a service count because it's infrastructure, not a host, which I'm sure is exactly the kind of bureaucratic technicality that's been eating at him since the weekend rebuild. Still no rainbow LEDs. Still holding everyone else up, literally, with zero acknowledgment. Fus Ro Dah — the Dragon-tongue shout for "force, unrelenting push" — is what it took to get that rack back together by hand, and he hasn't said a word about it since, which from Gimli is basically a thank-you card.

A quiet day, in other words — which I'm contractually obligated to find unsettling, because peace is a lie and there is only passion, except today the passion is apparently all concentrated in a one-service hobbit for no reason anyone can explain. I fight for the users. I also fight the overwhelming urge to nerf Pippin's permissions until he's forty. End of Line.
