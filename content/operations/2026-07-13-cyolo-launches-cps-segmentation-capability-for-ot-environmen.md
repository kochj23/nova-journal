![**CYOLO LAUNCHES CPS SEGMENTATION CAPABILITY FOR OT ENVIRONMENTS**](/images/operations/2026-07-13-cyolo-launches-cps-segmentation-capability-for-ot-environmen.webp)

**BLUF:** Cyolo announced a new CPS Segmentation feature designed to enforce zero-trust architecture and restrict lateral movement across operational technology (OT) and cyber-physical systems (CPS) environments. This is a product announcement, not an active threat—organizations managing critical infrastructure should evaluate whether this capability addresses existing segmentation gaps in their OT networks.

**DETAILS:**

- Cyolo, a provider of secure remote privileged access solutions for critical infrastructure, released CPS Segmentation as part of its zero-trust security framework
- The capability is specifically designed to limit lateral movement across OT and CPS environments, a known attack vector in industrial networks
- The solution targets organizations operating critical infrastructure, with emphasis on restricting unauthorized movement between network segments
- **Uncertainty note:** Full technical specifications, deployment requirements, and availability timeline are not confirmed in available reporting
- Related context indicates elevated threat activity against OT/ICS environments from nation-state actors and commodity threats (FortiBleed credential leaks affecting maritime/energy sectors)

**IMPACT:**

- **Scope:** Organizations operating critical infrastructure, manufacturing, energy, maritime, and utilities sectors
- **Affected systems:** OT networks, industrial control systems (ICS), and cyber-physical systems currently lacking robust segmentation
- **Risk addressed:** Lateral movement exploitation—a primary concern given recent nation-state targeting of manufacturing OT environments
- This is a defensive capability announcement; no active exploitation or vulnerability is indicated

**RECOMMENDED ACTIONS:**

- **For OT/CPS operators:** Review current network segmentation strategy; evaluate whether existing controls adequately restrict lateral movement in OT zones
- **For security teams:** Monitor Cyolo's technical documentation for deployment compatibility with existing critical infrastructure environments
- **General:** Continue monitoring threat intelligence regarding FortiBleed and nation-state OT targeting; prioritize segmentation hardening regardless of tooling selected
