![**US ADMINISTRATION RESTRICTS FOREIGN-PRODUCED ROBOTS; FCC BLOCKS IMPORTS OVER CRITICAL INFRASTRUCTURE CYBER RISK**](/images/operations/2026-07-31-us-administration-restricts-foreign-produced-robots-fcc-bloc.webp)

**BLUF:** The U.S. administration has determined that all foreign-produced advanced robotic devices pose an unacceptable risk to national security. The FCC has blocked imports of foreign-produced robots and power inverters, with specific action targeting Chinese-manufactured humanoid robots. Threat vector: cyberattacks, espionage, and remote manipulation of U.S. critical infrastructure. Organizations operating or procuring robotics systems should audit current deployments, particularly in critical sectors.

**DETAILS:**

- U.S. administration issued a determination that foreign-produced advanced robotic devices represent an unacceptable national security risk due to potential exposure to cyberattacks, espionage, and remote manipulation.
- FCC has taken regulatory action to block imports of new foreign-produced robots and power inverters, with explicit focus on humanoid robots originating in China.
- Threat model centers on compromise of robotic systems used in critical infrastructure, with potential for remote command execution and physical disruption.
- Related context: Russian state-sponsored and pro-Russia hacktivist groups are actively targeting U.S. and allied critical infrastructure (utilities, energy, logistics) via cyber means; foreign robotic systems could broaden attack surface.
- Scope of "foreign-produced advanced robotic devices" not fully detailed in available material; interpretation likely includes industrial automation, humanoid systems, and autonomous platforms with network connectivity.

**IMPACT:**

- **Scope:** Domestic operations and procurement pipelines for robotics in critical infrastructure sectors (energy, water, manufacturing, logistics, defense).
- **Affected organizations:** U.S. companies and agencies operating, installing, or procuring robotic systems from foreign manufacturers; supply chain integrators; critical infrastructure operators.
- **Risk level:** HIGH — compromised robotic systems could enable physical sabotage, sensor spoofing, or as pivot points into operational networks.
- **Timeline:** FCC action is current; organizations may face import restrictions and procurement delays.

**RECOMMENDED ACTIONS:**

- **Immediate:** Inventory all robotic systems in critical infrastructure and operational environments; identify country of origin and network connectivity status.
- **Short-term:** Audit existing foreign-produced robotic deployments for network segmentation, authentication controls, and update/patch mechanisms; consider air-gapping systems where operationally feasible.
- **Strategic:** Develop procurement policy to favor domestically produced or allied-nation robotic platforms for new critical infrastructure investments; coordinate with sector ISACs (Electricity, Water, Manufacturing) on remediation timelines.
- **Reporting:** Monitor FCC and CISA advisories for clarifications on scope (specific device categories, manufacturers, compliance timelines).

**SOURCES:**

- U.S. Administration directive on foreign robotic systems (date/specific agency not specified in available material).
- FCC import restrictions on foreign-produced robots and power inverters.
- CISA alerts re: pro-Russia hacktivist campaigns against U.S. critical infrastructure.
- Media reporting: SecurityWeek, The Hacker News (humanoid robot ban, China targeting); related industrial cyber threat assessments.
