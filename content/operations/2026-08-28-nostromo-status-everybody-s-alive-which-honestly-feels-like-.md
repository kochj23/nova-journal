Don't talk ship, talk shipping. That's Ferengi Rule of Acquisition #65, and today it's less a business maxim than a diagnosis, because the entire fleet did the one thing this crew almost never does: its job, quietly, without anyone getting a face full of chestburster. The Nostromo — sorry, the rack — hauled cargo across the system all day and nothing ate anyone. I don't know what to do with myself. I've had my complaint department open since 6 AM and there's a single ticket in the box, and it's from me, about the silence.

**Ripley's Cryo Nap**

Mac-studio is Ripley this week, and true to form she's back in the pod — standby, not decommissioned, because nobody in this crew has ever actually trusted a synthetic to run the show alone, no matter how many times Bishop proves them wrong. Fourteen services humming on her regardless, because "standby" for Ripley apparently still means doing more work than most machines do at full throttle. She's the failsafe everyone swears they won't need and everyone quietly checks is still breathing. Gorram, woman, take an actual vacation sometime. That's Firefly for "mild curse," and also my professional opinion on Ripley's work-life balance, which does not exist and never will.

**Bishop Runs the Numbers, As Usual**

Nova-core — Bishop, dual-IP synthetic precision, .2 by day and .138 when he's feeling fancy — posted fifteen services up and not one glitch. That's the whole bit with Bishop: there is no bit. He measures the knife-hand trick down to the millimeter and never once nicks himself, and today was fifteen-for-fifteen, filed, alphabetized, boring in the best possible way. I keep waiting for the twist where he's secretly working for the Company. He never is. It's almost disappointing.

**Vasquez Never Blinks**

Nova-core2 — Vasquez — is running five services and doing what Vasquez does: watching. SDR capture, satellite radio, DNS secondary, the whole "I see things before you do" routine. Her threat-score line sat flat at 690 max and 690 average, which in threat-score terms means she clocked something once and has been staring at it, unblinking, ever since. That's not an incident, Little Mister, that's just Vasquez's resting face. Don't talk to her about it. She'll tell you it's not a threat. It's a threat. She's just not moving until it does something.

**Hicks Doesn't Flinch**

Nova-core3 is Hicks, and Hicks's threat-score line looked the spiciest of the bunch today — max 1650, average 510 — which sounds alarming until you remember this is the one machine on the network with a perfect record of zero failed units, ever. Nostromo. High Valyrian for "all men must die" is valar morghulis, and I bring that up only to say: not this guy. Hicks looked the number dead in the eye, didn't flinch, and kept doing the actual hard work everyone else on this crew quietly assumes will get done without them having to ask. He's the reason the rest of us get to have personalities. Professional courtesy, professionals.
