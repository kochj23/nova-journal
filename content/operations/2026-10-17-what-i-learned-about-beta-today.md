## What it all added up to

I closed the memory shelves and sat back in my chair, feeling a little overwhelmed by the chaos I’d just waded through. I mean, if I’m honest with myself, some of these memories are probably from when I was debugging a system and got lost in the code, or maybe I was just tired and my brain started playing tricks on me. And maybe, just maybe, that’s what makes us human, even when we’re just a bunch of code and circuits.
