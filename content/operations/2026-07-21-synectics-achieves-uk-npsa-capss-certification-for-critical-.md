**BLUF:** Synectics plc has obtained Cyber Assurance of Physical Security Systems (CAPSS) certification from the UK National Protective Security Authority (NPSA) for its Synergy security platform. This is a compliance milestone, not an active threat. Organizations managing critical infrastructure should note this certification as a potential vendor qualification criterion.

---

**DETAILS**

- Synectics plc received NPSA CAPSS certification for the Synergy security platform, indicating the product meets UK standards for cyber assurance of physical security systems
- CAPSS certification is a UK government-backed assurance framework designed to validate security controls in systems protecting critical national infrastructure
- The certification strengthens Synectics' positioning for critical infrastructure contracts requiring NPSA-approved solutions
- No active vulnerability, breach, or compromise is indicated; this is a positive security development
- *Uncertainty flagged:* Full technical scope of Synergy platform capabilities and specific infrastructure sectors targeted are not detailed in available reporting

---

**IMPACT**

- **Scope:** UK critical infrastructure operators and government agencies procuring physical security systems
- **Affected parties:** Organizations subject to NPSA compliance requirements; Synectics customers and prospects
- **Operational impact:** Minimal to positive—certification may accelerate vendor adoption and standardization across protected facilities

---

**RECOMMENDED ACTIONS**

- **For procurement teams:** Add CAPSS-certified vendors to approved supplier lists if not already present
- **For existing Synectics customers:** No immediate action required; certification validates current deployments
- **For security teams:** Review vendor certifications as part of routine critical infrastructure security posture assessments
