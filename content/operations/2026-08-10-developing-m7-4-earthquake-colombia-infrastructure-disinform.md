**BLUF:** M7.4 earthquake struck 5 km east of San José del Palmar, Colombia, at 107 km depth (coordinates 4.903°N, 76.189°W). No confirmed critical infrastructure compromise or cascade failures as of initial reporting. Security concern: recent Brazil emergency alert system abuse and documented scammer exploitation of prior regional quakes create elevated risk for coordinated disinformation, false emergency alerts, and supply-chain disruption during response. Recommend: monitor official Colombian emergency channels for integrity; flag unverified crisis alerts in media/social; coordinate with Colombia's political transition team (recent right-wing election shift) on continuity messaging.

**DETAILS**
- **Confirmed seismic event:** M7.4 magnitude, 107 km depth, 5 km E of San José del Palmar (Chocó Dept., Colombia). Timestamp: 2026-08-10. Shallow-to-moderate depth consistent with intraslab rupture in Nazca plate subduction zone.
- **Infrastructure status:** No confirmed failure reports in initial period. Colombian seismic monitoring networks operational.
- **Disinformation precursor pattern:** Brazil emergency warning system exploited for unauthorized nationwide alert (June 2026); documented scammer campaigns activated within hours of Venezuela/Ecuador quakes (2026); Colombian political environment unstable post-election (narrow right-wing victory). Risk: false "critical infrastructure down" claims may spread faster than official all-clear.
- **Regional cascade risk:** Japan M7.1 earthquake (recent) coincided with active cyber-threat environment targeting critical infrastructure. No causal link confirmed, but temporal clustering noted in threat intel. Colombia's power, water, telecom systems should be monitored for opportunistic probing.

**IMPACT**
- **Geography:** Chocó Department (northwest Colombia, ~500k residents). Primary impact: Buenaventura port region (~400 km S), major Pacific terminus for Colombian trade. Secondary: coffee-growing regions (Eje Cafetero) ~200 km SE. Tertiary: Medellín infrastructure (~300 km E).
- **Scope:** Critical infrastructure potentially affected: port operations, power distribution, communications backbone. Electoral/political sensitivity: new government coordination during crisis will test institutional resilience.
- **Threat vectors:** Scammers posing as authorities; false emergency alerts on WhatsApp/Telegram; supply-chain disruption exploited for price manipulation.
