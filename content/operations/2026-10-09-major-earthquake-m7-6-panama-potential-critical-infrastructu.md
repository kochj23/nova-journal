**BLUF:** A magnitude 7.6 earthquake struck 10 km west-southwest of Pitaloza Arriba, Panama at 10 km depth. Immediate concern: Panama hosts critical hemisphere-spanning fiber routes, data centers, and undersea cables. Infrastructure damage assessment and continuity status unknown at this hour; activate regional resilience coordination NOW. No cybersecurity incident confirmed; this is infrastructure risk.

---

**DETAILS**

- **Confirmed event:** M7.6 earthquake, depth 10.0 km, epicenter 7.599°N, 80.757°W (10 km WSW of Pitaloza Arriba, Panama)
- **Timing:** Event occurred as of alert generation; no aftershock magnitude or count data available yet
- **Regional significance:** Epicenter is inland Panama, not coastal; damage radius likely 50+ km, potentially affecting population centers and infrastructure corridors
- **Critical infrastructure at risk:** Panama is a critical nexus for transatlantic/transpacific fiber, houses Amazon/Google/Microsoft edge infrastructure, hosts major carrier POPs, and data centers serving Americas. Undersea cables (SAC, PAC, others) may face disruption if landing stations damaged

---

**IMPACT**

- **Scope:** Uncertain. M7.6 can cause severe structural damage (USGS intensity VI–VIII+ in near zone); secondary risks include power grid outages, water system damage, and transport corridor disruption
- **Digital infrastructure:** No confirmed outages reported yet. Underwater cable landing stations near coastal areas at potential risk. Data center operations status unknown
- **Regional connectivity:** If major fiber routes or POPs damaged, potential cascading latency/outage effects for Cloud services, financial networks, and hemispheric connectivity
- **Affected populations/systems:** Southern Panama region; extent depends on structural resilience of local infrastructure

---

**RECOMMENDED ACTIONS**

- **Immediate (next 2 hours):** Check status of Panama-hosted critical infrastructure (AWS, Google, Microsoft regional POPs; cable landing stations; carrier hubs)
- **Coordinate with regional ISPs/infrastructure operators:** Request damage reports, restoration ETAs, failover activation status
- **Monitor USGS/seismic networks:** Aftershock risk; larger event cannot be ruled out in next 72 hours
- **Issue infrastructure resilience alert** to cloud customers and cross-border financial networks operating via Panama routes
- **Do NOT elevate to cyber incident** unless confirmed service impact emerges
