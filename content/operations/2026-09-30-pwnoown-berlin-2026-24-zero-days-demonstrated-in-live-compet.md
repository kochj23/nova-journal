- **Event scope:** Pwn2Own Berlin 2026 (22 research teams, live stage demonstrations) awarded **$523,000 for 24 unique 0-days** across four categories: AI Databases, Coding Agents, Local Inference systems, and NVIDIA products.

- **Categories attacked:** Vulnerability distribution spans emerging attack surface (AI/ML inference, LLM-based coding assistants) plus established NVIDIA infrastructure — indicates systematic targeting of the AI/ML stack.
- **Vendor notification:** ZDI coordinates 90-day responsible disclosure with affected vendors. Specific CVE assignments, patches, and public advisory dates not yet published in available sources.
