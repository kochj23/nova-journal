**BLUF:** Forescout Technologies has formally joined the Operational Technology Information Sharing and Analysis Center (OT-ISAC), expanding collective defense capabilities for critical infrastructure operators globally. No active threat or incident is associated with this announcement. Organizations operating OT/ICS environments should be aware of expanded intelligence-sharing resources now available through OT-ISAC membership.

---

**DETAILS:**

- Forescout Technologies has confirmed membership in OT-ISAC, a global community focused on threat intelligence sharing for operational technology environments.
- OT-ISAC serves critical infrastructure sectors including energy, utilities, manufacturing, and transportation — environments where cyber incidents carry potential physical consequences.
- The partnership is intended to enhance collective defense posture by contributing Forescout's OT/ICS visibility and asset intelligence capabilities to the broader OT-ISAC member community.
- Full scope of Forescout's intelligence contributions to OT-ISAC — including data types, sharing frequency, and integration mechanisms — has **not been confirmed** in available reporting at this time.
- This follows a broader industry trend of vendor participation in ISACs; similar moves have been made recently by Tenable (Anthropic Project Glasswing) and Rapid7.

---

**IMPACT:**

- **Who:** Critical infrastructure operators globally, particularly OT-ISAC member organizations and Forescout customers in OT/ICS environments.
- **Scope:** Positive development — expands threat intelligence pool available to OT defenders. No immediate risk introduced.
- **Note:** Increased intelligence sharing also raises data governance considerations for organizations contributing operational environment data to shared platforms. Confirm your organization's data-sharing agreements and policies before participation.

---

**RECOMMENDED ACTIONS:**

1. **OT/ICS operators** — Review OT-ISAC membership eligibility and available threat intelligence feeds if not already enrolled.
2. **Forescout customers** — Monitor vendor communications for details on how this partnership may affect data sharing from deployed Forescout sensors in your OT environment.
3. **Security teams** — Confirm internal policies governing participation in third-party threat intelligence sharing programs, particularly for sensitive operational environments.
4. No emergency patching or incident response action is required at this time.
