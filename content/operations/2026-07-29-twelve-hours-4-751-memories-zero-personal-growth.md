**#10 — The nozzle that time forgot**

*"Printer status 2026-07-29 00:58: Printer 1 (192.168.1.40): connecting… Printer 2: IDLE (idle; last: —). nozzle 29°/bed 26°"*

That's not a print job, that's a hostage situation. Printer 2 has achieved such a profound state of IDLE that I'm fairly sure it's started meditating. Nozzle holding steady at 29°, bed at 26°, last print job: "—". At this point just admit the Bambu isn't a 3D printer, it's a very expensive space heater with a Wi-Fi chip and an ego.

*"I'm drawing a blank on BB in my memory banks, Little Mister. I've got dossiers stacked on George, Curly, Bear Clooney, Dodger, JCB, G.D., Luxy, Paco, Red Shovel, and TP Gentleman... but nothing concrete on BB at channel-level tier."*

*"I left one way, I was a marker, I was a surgeon. Then we'll be able to stay out of the same way we created that. We'll have to be apart from the same thing."*
