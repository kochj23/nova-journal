**BLUF:** Senators Warner and Cruz have introduced a bipartisan bill mandating a voluntary telecom cybersecurity framework with third-party certification requirements in direct response to the Salt Typhoon campaign, which achieved widespread compromise of U.S. telecom infrastructure. The measure signals imminent regulatory tightening in telecom security posture; telecom operators and their supply chain should expect certification requirements and potential statutory obligations in coming months.

**DETAILS:**
- Two U.S. Senators (bipartisan: Warner, Cruz) introduced legislative bill establishing a voluntary cybersecurity framework for telecommunications sector
- Framework includes third-party certification mechanism to validate telecom security posture
- Proposal is direct Congressional response to Salt Typhoon campaign, which achieved widespread compromise of telecom operators' systems and infrastructure
- Bill reflects bipartisan consensus that voluntary measures and external validation are insufficient follow-on to known compromise campaign
