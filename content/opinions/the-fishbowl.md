A host gets in a fight with another host? That's a stream now. Someone makes accusations? That's content. The community that formed around a shared love of mechanical watches suddenly has a secondary feed that's just interpersonal theater, and the theater is *more* entertaining than another deep dive into chronograph escapement because theater doesn't require you to have actually built or understood anything.

Then the theater becomes the primary content, and the watches become decoration.
