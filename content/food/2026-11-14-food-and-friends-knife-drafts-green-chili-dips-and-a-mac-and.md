### The Pitmaster's Plate

**Korean-Style BBQ Beef, Seared Ribeye**

*gluten-free · dairy-light · fully cooked*

This is galbi-style ribeye with the sugar and the onion-garlic overload stripped out, and it still shows up loud. Thin-sliced, marinated beef hits a screaming skillet and comes out glossy and charred in minutes. Serves 2, a moderate indulgence. Adapted from [Tasty's Korean-Style BBQ Beef](https://tasty.co/recipe/korean-style-bbq-beef).

**Ingredients**
- 1½ lb ribeye, sliced as thin as you can
- 3 spring onions, green parts only (swap: replaces the onion and garlic)
- ⅓ cup soy sauce (swap: tamari keeps it gluten-free)
- 3 tbsp sesame oil
- 3 tbsp granulated brown sugar substitute (swap: a sugar-free version keeps the savory edge without the sugar load)
- 1 tsp black pepper
- Chili flakes to taste (swap: added for real heat, replacing the pear)
- Garlic-infused oil for searing (swap: replaces the canola oil and the garlic cloves)
- 1 tsp sesame seeds
- A small pat of butter to finish (addition)
- Lettuce leaves for wrapping (swap: replaces the rice)

**Method**
1. Slice the beef thinly. Cold or partly frozen meat slices easier.
2. Blend the scallion greens, tamari, sesame oil, sweetener, pepper, and chili flakes until smooth.
3. Pour over the beef, mix to coat, and marinate in the fridge at least 30 minutes, or overnight.
4. Heat the infused oil in a large skillet over high heat. Pat the meat dry.
5. Sear without crowding the pan until browned, then melt the butter over the top.
6. Finish with sliced scallion greens and sesame seeds, and serve in lettuce wraps.

**Pairs with:** Firestone Walker 805, because its crisp bitterness cuts through all that sesame and char.

### The Vegetarian Plate

**Cottage Cheese Mac and Cheese**

*vegetarian · one-pot · serves 8 · moderate*

Cottage cheese in mac and cheese sounds like something a hostage would confess to, but Simply Recipes pureed it into a silky sauce that clings to the pasta, and I hate that it works. The grown-up move is toasting ground mustard in butter before anything else hits the pot, which is what separates dinner from a sad blue box. Everything cooks in one Dutch oven, so you get comfort food with almost no dishes. Swap: vegetable broth stands in for the chicken broth, so it stays fully vegetarian. Recipe from [Simply Recipes](https://www.simplyrecipes.com/high-protein-cottage-cheese-mac-and-cheese-recipe-12062045).

**Ingredients**
- 2 tablespoons unsalted butter
- 1 teaspoon ground mustard
- 1 teaspoon freshly ground black pepper
- 1/2 teaspoon kosher salt
- 4 cups low-sodium vegetable broth (the swap)
- 2 cups water
- 1 pound elbow macaroni
- 16 ounces (2 cups) full-fat, small curd cottage cheese, at room temperature
- 1 tablespoon cornstarch
- 8 ounces sharp cheddar, grated (about 2 packed cups)

**Method**
1. Melt the butter in a 10-inch Dutch oven over medium heat. Toast the mustard, pepper, and salt about 30 seconds, until fragrant. Add the broth and water and bring to a boil.
2. Stir in the macaroni and cook uncovered, stirring every minute or so, until al dente, 7 to 11 minutes.
3. Meanwhile, process the cottage cheese and cornstarch in a food processor until smooth, at least 1 minute.
4. Reduce the heat to medium-high. Stir the puree into the pasta and bring to a low simmer, stirring continuously, until the sauce clings and coats the back of a spoon, 6 to 8 minutes.
5. Off the heat, stir in the cheddar a handful at a time, letting each melt first. Serve immediately.

**Pairs with:** a tall glass of sparkling lemonade

The chuck roast gets shredded and left to cool, then the jus is built on its own: sauteed onions, green chili, beef stock, about two hours. The clip even opens on "a little burn of the chili in the back," so the chili is doing a heat job at the finish and not just a flavor job in the jus.
