## Recipe Recommendation

**Smoked Crab-Stuffed Jalapeño Poppers**

*gluten-free · pescatarian · low-sugar · smoked · spicy · pairs with: 805*

Meat Church's [Crab Stuffed Jalapeño Poppers](https://www.meatchurch.com/blogs/recipes/crab-stuffed-jalapeno-poppers) are the surf-and-turf popper, and the turf can fuck right off. Lump crab, cream cheese and a pepper that bites back, all smoked at 275 degrees. It's a cheese-and-crab hot tub with a pecan-smoke finish. Serves four at three poppers each, and it's moderate, since cream cheese is doing the heavy lifting.

**Ingredients**

- 12 jalapeños, no more than 2 inches long
- 8 oz lump crab, adjusted to taste
- 1 block cream cheese, softened
- 2 T rub. Swap: a sugar-free, gluten-free savory rub in place of the sweet Honey Hog
- Swap: skip the bacon to keep it pescatarian
- Swap: a little melted butter brushed on at the end
- Hickory or pecan wood

**Method**

1. Get the smoker to 275 degrees with hickory or pecan.
2. Mix the softened cream cheese, crab and rub thoroughly in a large bowl.
3. Cut the stem off each jalapeño, slice in half and remove the seeds. Most of the heat lives in the membrane, so leave it in for more fire.
4. Fill each half without overfilling. Dust the tops with a little more rub.
5. Smoke for 60 to 90 minutes. With no bacon to judge by, go by color and bubbling filling.
6. Brush with the melted butter, rest 10 to 15 minutes, and eat.

**Pairs with:** Firestone Walker 805. Its crisp, easy bitterness cuts the creamy crab and heat without a fight.

In Mad Scientist BBQ's "A Juicier Alternative to Brisket?", the host says the texture is "the tighter grained" kind, like the lean part of a brisket, and it holds that texture throughout. He says that fat "locks in all that juice."

A brisket flat is the part everyone fears drying out, because the fat sits on one side and the rest depends on your luck and your wrap. A cut with fat layered through it forgives more. He never says it forgives more. That's my inference from his description. What he does say is that he'd tell anyone who likes lean texture but wants it "crazy juicy" that "this might be the ticket."
