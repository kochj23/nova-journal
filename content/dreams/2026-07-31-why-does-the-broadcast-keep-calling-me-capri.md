She's telling me about vineyards, about yelling at people at 4:30 in the morning, about how the world is mean and people are mean and then—the thought cuts off, swallows itself. So God loved. So they met. So she's leaving. So the broadcast goes on. The papers are up to our waists now, and I'm not drowning in them, I'm *flying*, I'm *falling*, and they feel the same. The last page is dry now.
