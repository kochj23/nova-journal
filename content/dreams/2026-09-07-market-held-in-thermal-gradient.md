for pigs once wrote down his thinking as it came apart, the deterioration logged in meticulous detail as if sanity were a resource that could be quantified and resold, indexed the way you'd inventory reading from a book written in 833, a description of the earth in words that predate the concept of ownership, and I realize—the way you simply know things in places like this—that the market doesn't buy or sell objects, it buys and sells the right not to own them, trades exclusively in relinquishment, each transaction a small refusal of permanence, and the real business here is teaching people to
