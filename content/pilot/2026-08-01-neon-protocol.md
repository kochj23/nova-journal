## **COLD OPEN**

**INT. WAREHOUSE - NIGHT**

Rain. Exposed brick. Industrial lighting casts long shadows across concrete.

A FIGURE moves through darkness, phone screen glowing—scrolling through TikTok. Casual. Unaware.

**MAYA CHEN (28, sharp-eyed, wearing vintage Comme des Garçons)** stands twelve feet away, watching. Her hand rests on her service weapon.

**MAYA**  
(into earpiece)  
Still scrolling. No awareness of the room.

**DISPATCH (V.O.)**  
(crackling)  
Backup is three minutes out.

Maya steps forward. Her footstep echoes.

The FIGURE spins. **JAMES TORRES (19, lanky, terrified)**. Holding a stolen Rolex. Not a gun.

**MAYA**  
Don't move.

**JAMES**  
Officer—

**MAYA**  
Hands. Now.

He complies. The Rolex clatters to concrete.

Silence. Rain on the skylight.

Then—**WHUMP-WHUMP-WHUMP.**

A DRONE descends through the skylight. Red and black. Professional.

**MAYA**  
What the—

The drone releases a canister. Smoke floods the warehouse—thick, white, choking.

Maya draws. Fires. Misses. The drone pivots. Vanishes through the skylight.

When the smoke clears, James Torres is gone.

Maya stands alone, coughing, weapon raised at nothing.

**MAYA (CONT'D)**  
(into earpiece)  
Suspect fled. Hostile aerial vehicle.

**DISPATCH (V.O.)**  
(pause)  
Copy that, Detective. First one we've heard of.

Maya stares at the skylight. Rain falls through.

**FADE TO BLACK.**
