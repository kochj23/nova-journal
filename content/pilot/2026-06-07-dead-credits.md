## COLD OPEN

**FADE IN:**

**INT. HARGROVE ACADEMY - SERVER ROOM - NIGHT**

Rows of humming servers cast a blue-white glow. The room is cold enough to see breath. A HAND reaches into frame, plugging a small USB device into a rack-mounted server. The hand belongs to someone wearing a Hargrove Academy lanyard — but we can't see the face.

A progress bar crawls across a laptop screen propped on a server shelf.

**LAPTOP SCREEN INSERT:** *"OMS MIRROR — TRANSFERRING... 847 FILES..."*

The bar hits 100%.

The hand yanks the USB out.

**FOOTSTEPS** echo somewhere beyond the server room door.

The laptop snaps shut. Darkness.

**INT. HARGROVE ACADEMY - HALLWAY - CONTINUOUS**

DETECTIVE NORA VÁSQUEZ, 38, moves through the fluorescent-lit corridor like she owns it — which she doesn't. She's holding a paper coffee cup with "NORA" misspelled as "LAURA." Dark circles under sharp eyes. A blazer that was pressed three days ago.

Beside her: PRINCIPAL ALDOUS FENN, 61, a man who irons his pocket squares and regrets it immediately when anyone notices.

**FENN**
*(whispering urgently)*
I want to be very clear, Detective. This is a school. Whatever happened tonight, the students cannot —

**VÁSQUEZ**
Where's the body, Mr. Fenn?

**FENN**
*(flinching at the word)*
The — yes. This way.

---

**INT. HARGROVE ACADEMY - FINANCIAL AID OFFICE - CONTINUOUS**

A modest office. Filing cabinets. A motivational poster: *"YOUR FUTURE STARTS HERE."* Ironic, given that PROFESSOR GERALD MAST, 55, is slumped face-down on his desk, very much without a future.

Vásquez steps inside. Pulls on a latex glove. Touches nothing yet — just reads the room.

On the desk beside Mast's outstretched arm: a half-eaten granola bar. A coffee mug with the Hargrove crest. And a sticky note that reads, in urgent block letters:

**INSERT — STICKY NOTE:** *"THEY'RE RUNNING IT AGAIN. CHECK THE LOAN FILES — C."*

**VÁSQUEZ**
Who found him?

**FENN**
Janitor. Around eleven. I came immediately. I haven't — I didn't touch —

**VÁSQUEZ**

**FENN**
Financial Aid Director. Fifteen years.

**VÁSQUEZ**
He have enemies?

Fenn opens his mouth. Closes it.

**FENN**
He was a *financial aid director*, Detective. At a *prep school*. He managed loan deferments and scholarship disbursements. He wasn't exactly —

**VÁSQUEZ**
Everybody's got enemies, Mr. Fenn.

She looks at the sticky note again. At the initial. *C.*

**VÁSQUEZ (CONT'D)**
*(mostly to herself)*
What were you running, Gerald?

The fluorescent light above FLICKERS once.

Then holds.

## ACT ONE

**INT. HARGROVE ACADEMY - MAIN ENTRANCE - MORNING**

Hargrove Academy looks like money that's been here long enough to forget it's money. Stone archways. Ivy. A bronze plaque: *"EDUCATING LEADERS SINCE 1887."*

Vásquez leans against a pillar, watching. She's on her second coffee. Still misspelled.

A TOWN CAR pulls up. Out steps CASEY DRUMMOND, 17, in a Hargrove blazer that fits her like a costume she hasn't grown into yet. She's carrying three different notebooks and a tablet. She moves fast, head down, like she's always late for something.

She steps forward.

**FELIX**

**FENN**

**VÁSQUEZ**

**FENN**

**VÁSQUEZ**

**FENN**

**VÁSQUEZ**
Gerald Mast is dead. His office was accessed last night. Someone went into your server room.

**VÁSQUEZ (CONT'D)**
The files, Mr. Fenn.

**INT. HARGROVE ACADEMY - COMPUTER SCIENCE CLASSROOM - DAY**

**CASEY**

**FELIX**
