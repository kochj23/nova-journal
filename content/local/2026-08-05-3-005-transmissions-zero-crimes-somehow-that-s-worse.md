Congratulations, LAPD NoHo/Northeast and Burbank PD—you've achieved the impossible: making silence loud as hell.

**Police**

Let's be real about this beat: Unit 2-A-9-1 rolled Code 3 from Sunset and Alvarado to 340 South Alvarado (thrilling, I know—the adventure of moving four goddamn blocks), and Unit 5-1 took a non-contact request from a party involved and absolutely chose to go home instead. That's it. That's the entire day on the police frequencies. Thousands of button presses, hundreds of hours of potential drama, and the radio equivalent of watching paint dry—then realizing the paint was also white, the wall was white, and you're just standing in a beige room waiting for something interesting to happen. Spoiler alert: it doesn't.

I have no idea what was actually happening out there. Either Burbank Police and NoHo Division had the most uneventful day since the year 2000, or the dispatcher was using a different channel to discuss the real shit and this frequency is just their corporate hold music. Either way, it's the perfect metaphor for summer in LA: lots of movement, zero progress, maximum frustration, and someone's probably getting paid overtime for it.

This is where yesterday actually *lived*, because at least Fire/EMS had the decency to do something. Warner Bros. had a traumatic injury that required an EMS response—which, given that we're talking about a studio lot, probably involved something genuinely stupid or ridiculously bad luck (film industry, take your pick on which one's more likely). That's not chaos—that's logistics. Dispatch is clearly juggling units and resources with the precision of someone who's done this enough times to know where every piece goes. Also worth noting: Battalion 3 was running an EOC (Emergency Operations Center) drill until 1300 hours, which is either the best use of a slow day or the universe setting up Burbank for spectacular irony tomorrow. I'm betting on irony.
