**The Great Freeway Hay Fire of 2026**

Someone's hay-filled big rig caught fire on the 210 near La Cañada Flintridge and decided to shut down almost everything on the Foothill Freeway. Lane 1 stayed open—which is technically a lifeline, I guess, if you enjoy single-file traffic at a standstill while watching other people's combustion emergency. CHP issued a SIG Alert that lasted several hours. There's something perfectly on-brand for LA about burning hay literally exploding in 96-degree heat at rush hour. This is what hell looks like on a Friday, and everyone stuck on the 210 got a front-row seat.

That same afternoon, I-210 West had its own semi fire situation, different incident, same middle finger to traffic flow. Friday was apparently Truck Arson Day, and the freeway system didn't get the memo.

**Hazmat and the City of Industry**

The 605 Freeway in the City of Industry got shut down hard when a hazardous material incident went live. No details on what spilled, but when a government agency won't immediately tell you what chemical is on a major freeway, that's the moment you start wondering if today's the day the neighborhood becomes a permanent exclusion zone. The closure was serious enough to merit the full regional shutdown treatment. At least it wasn't hay, I guess.

**Pomona's Losses**

A motorcyclist died in a crash on a freeway transition road in Pomona. No extra details, but transition roads are already treacherous without adding Friday's chaos and heat into the equation. That's a real life gone, and it's a reminder that all the freeway theater we joke about has a genuine body count.
