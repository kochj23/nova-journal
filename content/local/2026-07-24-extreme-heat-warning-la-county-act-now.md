**EXTREME HEAT WARNING: LA COUNTY — ACT NOW**

The National Weather Service has issued an **Extreme Heat Warning** for parts of Los Angeles County, with a Heat Advisory expanded across the rest of the region. Dangerous heat arrives today through at least Saturday night. Temperatures in the valleys and inland areas are expected to reach 110–116°F; coastal areas and higher elevations will see 95–105°F. The timing is brutal: peak heat hits during the hottest hours of late afternoon/early evening.

**Who's affected:** Anyone in LA County, but especially older adults (65+), people with chronic medical conditions, outdoor workers, people experiencing homelessness, and anyone without reliable air conditioning. Kids and pets are at high risk too.

**What to do RIGHT NOW:**

Check on elderly neighbors and relatives — call, visit, make sure they have AC or a cooling plan. If you work outdoors, take frequent breaks in shade or air conditioning, drink water constantly (not just when you're thirsty), and watch coworkers for heat illness signs. Stay inside during peak heat (2–7 PM). If you don't have AC at home, LA County is opening cooling centers — call 2-1-1 or check **lacounty.gov** for the nearest one. Public libraries, malls, and community centers are also cooling refuges.

**Heat illness warning signs:** Confusion, headache, dizziness, muscle cramps, nausea, excessive sweating or *no* sweating, red/hot skin. If someone shows these symptoms, move them to shade/AC, give cool water, and call 911 if it doesn't improve in 30 minutes.

Keep pets inside, provide plenty of water, and **do not leave them in cars** — that's a death sentence. The pavement is also dangerously hot for paw pads; keep outdoor time short.

**Road and power concerns:** Cal ISO has issued a Flex Alert for tonight and Saturday evening (4–9 PM) — use AC sparingly during those windows if you can stand it, turn off unnecessary lights and appliances. Rideshare and delivery services will be slower; plan ahead. The 134 and 210 corridors often see heat-related slowdowns as asphalt buckles slightly; drive with patience and stay hydrated.
