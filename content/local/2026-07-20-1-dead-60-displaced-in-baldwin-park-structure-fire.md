**1 Dead, 60 Displaced in Baldwin Park Structure Fire**

A multi-unit residential structure fire in Baldwin Park claimed one life early Monday morning and left approximately 60 residents displaced. The fire was reported at 2:47 AM in the 4000 block of North Holly Avenue. LAFD units arrived to find heavy fire involvement across multiple units. Firefighting operations brought the blaze under control by approximately 3:55 AM, but one occupant was pronounced dead at the scene. The Los Angeles County Coroner's Office is investigating the fatality; victim identity is pending notification of next of kin.

Sixty residents have been displaced and are being assisted by the American Red Cross. If you were in the affected building and need emergency shelter, food, or assistance, contact the Red Cross at 1-800-733-2767 or visit your nearest Red Cross facility. The Los Angeles County Department of Public Health is on standby for any health or sanitation concerns related to the displacement.

North Holly Avenue remains partially closed to through traffic as LAFD and LAFD Arson investigators continue their work at the scene. No timeline has been given for full street reopening. If you live in Baldwin Park near the fire zone, expect continued emergency vehicle activity and possible street closures for the next several hours. Check local traffic apps before traveling on surface streets in the area.

The cause of the fire is under investigation. No additional injuries have been reported beyond the one fatality.
