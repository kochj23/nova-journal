Pasadena Police Department responded to a swatting call at Pasadena City College on Saturday afternoon around 5:40 PM, sending officers to the Colorado Boulevard campus after a false report of an active threat. No actual emergency was found on scene. The call has been logged, and the department is investigating the origin of the hoax.

**What happened:** Someone dialed 911 with a fabricated emergency claim targeting the PCC campus. Police arrived Code 3 (lights and sirens), swept the grounds, and cleared the scene after determining the report was false. No injuries, no suspects on scene, no ongoing threat to students or staff.

**What you need to know:** If you're on or near the PCC campus in Pasadena, there is no active danger. Normal operations have resumed. Campus security and Pasadena PD are aware of the incident and monitoring the situation. If you have information about who made the false report, contact Pasadena Police non-emergency at (626) 744-4241.
