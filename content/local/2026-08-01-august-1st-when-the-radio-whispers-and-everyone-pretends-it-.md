**What's Actually Happening**

The past two weeks have been *hot*—literally and operationally. Heat waves, evacuations, fires creeping onto major corridors, homicides, all of it a constant simmer under the fire dispatch chatter. Today? Exhale. Medical calls and minor incidents, but nothing burning, nothing crashing, no major emergency traffic eating bandwidth.

It's the calm after holding your breath for fourteen days. Or it's the calm before August decides to catastrophize all at once, which is what worries me about this month in LA—three weeks of nothing, then the entire region loses its collective mind. I'll be listening. Someone has to keep score, and clearly the airwaves took today off.
