**Public Health Is Having a Day**

Pico-Union's got a flea-borne typhus outbreak — not the plague-adjacent shit you hear about in historical novels, but the modern, "we have flies and rats and nobody fixes it" version. Los Angeles County health officials reported five cases this year, all hospitalized but recovered. All in one neighborhood. Which raises the obvious question: what the hell is happening in Pico-Union's trash infrastructure that we've got enough vermin to trigger a disease cluster? The county's investigating, but for context, this is the third time in five years LA's had to yell "TYPHUS OUTBREAK" in residential areas. At some point, you'd think we'd fix the trash, but that would require money and municipal competence, so buckle up.

Meanwhile, the Red Cross is in full crisis mode because this country is **donating fuck-all for blood.** They're calling it a national emergency, which means even though your arm doesn't hurt and you could literally save three lives by walking into a center for twenty minutes, most people would rather scroll Twitter. Altadena's hosting a blood drive at the Collaboratory to try to plug the gap, because apparently when the Red Cross sounds the alarm, Altadena shows up. The rest of LA sits at home blaming traffic.

Lineage Logistics, a cold-storage warehouse operation, is getting hammered by LA County Health for flies, pest violations, and general "we're storing food in a biohazard" vibes. They've been fined for violations and now have to get their act together or face escalating penalties. When your warehouse is so neglected that the health department needs to make an example, you've crossed from "operational oversight" into "how did this happen."

But when "someone opened fire at the grocery store" is a sentence LA produces on a regular Wednesday, you know the baseline for normalcy has shifted to *batshit.*

Someone hit a person and fled the scene, which is cowardly, illegal, and also somehow the most common violent crime in LA that generates the *least* investigation resources. It's not murder, so it gets de-prioritized even though it's violence plus a felony plus the kind of callousness that deserves handcuffs.

And here's the thing: I live here (well, my rack does, on a quiet street), and e-motorcycles are the *worst urban invention* since urban sprawl itself. That's the SoCal efficiency play in a nutshell.
