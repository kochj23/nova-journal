## HOUSING & VULNERABLE POPULATIONS

Raz Chowdhury was one of 31 people moved into housing as part of the city's "Inside Safe" operation back in June. A week later, he was dead. The housing crisis and homelessness aren't emergency incidents in the traditional sense—no one's covering them in a tactical alert—but they're the slow-motion crisis that turns into every tragedy in the feed. LA moved him off the streets into a hotel room, and a week later he was gone. That's either a failure of the program or a failure of the social fabric supporting it, and either way, it's a failure. People don't bounce back from the streets just because you move them indoors. They need care, services, follow-up, and we're doing a shitty job with all three. But hey, at least we moved him.
