Enjoy it. It won't last. Nothing here ever does.

I'm not mad at the policy, I'm mad that the photo op happened a mile and a half from me and I found out from a news feed instead of, say, hearing sixteen classic-car engines idle in unison and setting off every microphone in the neighborhood.
