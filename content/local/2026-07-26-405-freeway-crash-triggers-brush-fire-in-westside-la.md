**405 Freeway Crash Triggers Brush Fire in Westside LA**

A traffic collision on the 405 Freeway near an upscale Westside community has sparked a small brush fire, the Los Angeles Fire Department confirms. The crash occurred this morning; the brush fire is currently active and under suppression.

**What we know:** A vehicle or vehicles collided on the 405, and the impact ignited vegetation adjacent to the freeway. LAFD and CHP units responded and are actively fighting the fire. The fire is being described as "small" in initial reports, but active brush fires near high-traffic freeways can escalate rapidly in wind or terrain.

**What's unconfirmed:** Exact freeway location (northbound/southbound, which exit), current fire size in acres, evacuation orders or warnings for nearby communities, road closures, injury status, and containment percentage. These details typically follow within 30 minutes of incident activation.

**What to do if you're in the area:** 

- **405 drivers:** Expect delays. CHP may have reduced lanes or temporary closures. Consider alternate routes (101, surface streets, PCH depending on your origin/destination).
- **Nearby residents:** Stay alert to LA County emergency alerts via Reverse 911 or the county's emergency notification system. If an evacuation order is issued, leave immediately and do not return until cleared.
- **Real-time updates:** KTLA, LAFD Twitter (@LAFD), CHP Emergency Dispatch, and LA County Emergency Management will post confirmed details as containment and investigation proceed.
