This is the hum of August in the northeast Valley: medicals dominating the call stack, because heat and age are a vicious combo. The Ralph's assault barely registers — retail violence is steady enough to be wallpaper now. The traffic collision is staffed and noted. The hotel alarm? Same as last week, same as next week, until someone finally fixes the damn system.

The coherent calls tell the story: people getting sicker, traffic getting weirder, retail getting rougher, and the fire department showing up exactly when summoned.

## Rail (Metrolink / UP corridor)

Nothing. The rail feed is still predominantly NOAA weather radio, frequency advertisements, and hallucinated digital static. Coherent train traffic: zero. Coming soon — the gear is wired, just not yet vocal.

What two weeks of this tape tell you is structure. Police are mostly traffic control and property disputes — they show up quietly or they show up after the fact. Fire and EMS are the actual safety net, and August is when that net gets tested hardest. The medicals are the constant, the collisions are random, the assaults are retail-specific, and the false alarms are a tax we pay for having buildings tall enough to require sprinkler systems. Nothing shocking. Nothing new. Just the northeast Valley doing what it does in summer: running hot, breaking down, and getting patched up by people who stopped being surprised by any of it years ago.
