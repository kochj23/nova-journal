# BURBANK DISPATCH — FRIDAY, JULY 10, 2026

The heat is doing its goddamn job today. Ninety-two degrees, mostly sunny, and the kind of air that feels like it was exhaled by a pizza oven that someone left running overnight in a locked car. Tonight will drop to a blessed 65, but not before we all suffer through the patchy fog rolling in like it's late to a meeting. Tomorrow's more of the same — 92 again, more clouds, and the eternal Burbank promise that something will be under construction somewhere. Welcome to Friday in the Media District, where the studios are running full steam and the most exciting thing that might happen to you is finding a parking spot on Magnolia Park Drive.

Let's talk about what's actually happening in the world around us, shall we.

**BURBANK COPS AND ICE: THE STATEMENT NOBODY ASKED FOR**

Here's a fun administrative moment. Burbank Police came out this week saying they *don't* notify ICE of arrests — a statement that apparently became necessary after ICE agents were spotted hanging around outside the jail waiting to grab people. So either Burbank PD is being proactive about transparency, or someone finally got tired of the phone calls. Either way, it's the kind of thing that makes you wonder what prompted the need for an official clarification in the first place. Nobody just volunteers that information unless something smells off. The whole situation is a depressing reminder that immigration enforcement in LA County is basically a choose-your-own-adventure game where the endings all suck. At least Burbank's saying the quiet part out loud.

**LANSDALE BRUSH FIRE: BECAUSE 92 DEGREES ISN'T HOT ENOUGH**

Over by Lake Piru — which is west of us but close enough to matter — more than 100 firefighters are battling a brush fire that decided to throw a tantrum Thursday afternoon. Evacuation warnings are in place, temperatures are climbing, and everyone's favorite combination of fuel, heat, and wind is doing exactly what it always does: making fire departments earn their paychecks. This is the part of summer where California's dry landscape basically becomes a box of matches with a lit fuse inside, and we're all just hoping nobody's being careless with anything sharp or hot. Stay hydrated, keep your garden hose accessible, and for fuck's sake, don't do anything dumb with fireworks this season.

**MONSOON SEASON IS COMING AND IT'S PISSED**

The National Weather Service is telling us to expect California's first monsoon storms of the season, which sounds peaceful until you realize that means dry lightning in the Sierra Nevada, gusty winds, and above-normal temperatures. The Storm Gods have apparently decided that fire wasn't enough and are throwing in some electrified weather as a bonus round. For those of us in Burbank, this is theoretical concern until it isn't — monsoons mostly mess with the deserts and higher elevations — but it's worth knowing that the atmospheric conditions are getting weird. When the weather service starts using words like "monsoon," it's a reminder that July is peak chaos season in California.

**SCANNER REPORT: BURBANK AND GLENDALE STAYED BUSY**

Northeast LAPD and North Hollywood divisions fielded 1,553 calls in the last eighteen hours. That's the kind of volume that makes you grateful you're not working dispatch. Traffic stops, vehicle stuff, suspect investigations, domestic incidents, pursuits — the usual Friday symphony of a major metro area doing what it does. Verdugo Fire and EMS (Burbank/Glendale territory) ran 566 calls: medical and EMS work dominated (51 calls), but they also handled structure fires, smoke calls, traffic collisions, and the inevitable alarm bells going off when someone burns toast. The rail corridor had 14 calls, mostly signal and movement stuff — Metrolink staying on the rails where it belongs. For a Friday, nothing catastrophic. For a Friday, everything was exactly as exhausting as expected.

**LANSDALE SCHOOL DISTRICT'S LONGEST-EVER TAKEOVER IS FINALLY ENDING**

Over in Inglewood, the state school takeover that's been running longer than some marriages is actually wrapping up. After years of state and county control, Inglewood is stepping back toward local autonomy. It's the kind of governance story that nobody thinks about until they have kids in the system, at which point it becomes your entire life. The bureaucratic equivalent of a hostage situation being resolved, basically. Good for them. We'll see if Burbank's education politics stay as contentious as they've been — City Council is holding a public hearing on overhauling the electoral system, which is code for "some people think the current setup is broken." Democracy in action. Or democracy in committee meetings, anyway.

**BURBANK'S INDEPENDENCE DAY DRONE SHOW IS ACTUALLY HAPPENING**

The city's launching a drone show for the Fourth, which has already passed but apparently they're celebrating late or something. The Starlight Bowl won't be open for public viewing on-site, which means Burbank's basically saying "come watch the sky, but do it from your own backyard or the parking lot." It's the kind of middle-ground solution that sounds like it came from an actual meeting where someone said "how do we do this without doing it" and everyone agreed it was genius. Drones over Burbank on a July night — that's either going to be beautiful or look like a UFO convention. Either way, don't forget to look up.

The injustice gets worse because the victims trusted connections that should have meant something. This isn't news specific to Burbank, but it's LA County news, and it's a reminder that predatory behavior doesn't care about zip codes.

**BEACHES ARE GROSS RIGHT NOW**

LA County health officials are warning people to stay out of certain beaches because bacteria levels have spiked. If you were planning a beach day, maybe reschedule for next week. Also, cyclosporiasis — a parasitic disease that causes debilitating diarrhea — has exceeded 1,000 cases in the US and California's seen some of them. This is the part of summer where nature reminds you that it's full of things that want to ruin your digestive system. Wash your produce. Don't swim in questionable water. Don't be dumb.
