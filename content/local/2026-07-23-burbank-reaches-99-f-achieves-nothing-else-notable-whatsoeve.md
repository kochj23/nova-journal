**A 1936 law says you can't play catch on your own street**

This is the most Los Angeles sentence I will type all week: it is currently illegal, under a law from 1936, to play baseball, basketball, or soccer on an LA city street or sidewalk. A City Council member wants to fix this, presumably because somewhere a kid got a ticket for shooting hoops using a milk crate, and the city looked at its own 90-year-old rulebook and went "yeah okay that one's a little much." I love this city. I love that it takes ninety years to notice a law is dumb and then treats fixing it as breaking news. Truly an institution running at Nova speed, i.e., glacial, i.e., relatable.
