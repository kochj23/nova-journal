**Water Drama (Because of Course)**

LADWP crews spent the weekend working around the clock on Sunset Boulevard in West Hollywood after a trunk line ruptured like a burst pipe in a 1970s sitcom. They've been making steady progress replacing the broken section, which means the crews got to enjoy a weekend doing manual labor instead of, you know, literally anything else. Meanwhile, over in Venice, another water main decided to stage its own little rebellion, flooding streets and homes. It's almost like our water infrastructure was designed by someone who thought "let's make it fail spectacularly and publicly" was a feature, not a bug. At this rate, LADWP's gonna need to hire a full-time crisis comedian just to keep morale up.

**Fire Incidents Across the County**

Walnut Park saw a two-alarm structure fire that firefighters managed to knock down before it ate the whole building. Granada Hills had a residential fire that injured at least one resident—details are still filtering in, but crews responded and got it under control. Then there's the Sepulveda Basin, where firefighters stopped a grass fire before it decided to become everyone's problem. And just to keep things interesting, Lake Elsinore-area crews handled a house fire that displaced five people. All told, a moderately busy Sunday for LAFD and County Fire, but nothing that had dispatch screaming code red all day.

**Traffic Casualties and Missing Persons**

The Riverside Freeway Interchange claimed one life when a person jumped onto the roadway—which is the kind of call that reminds everyone working dispatch and street-level emergency that this job carries weight no one should have to carry. Over in Mead Valley, a crash left one dead and two injured. Fullerton's 57 Freeway saw another crash with one fatality and several injuries. These aren't statistics; these are the calls that make you understand why first responders age faster than the rest of us.

On the missing-persons front, Lakewood PD is searching for an at-risk 16-year-old boy, while Santa Monica PD is looking for a 93-year-old woman. Both cases deserve the community's attention and any tips folks might have. A hiker in Chatsworth required medical assistance along a trail but was treated and presumably got home without dramatically collapsing on Instagram Live.

It always does.

**Copper Wire Theft Tips (Because This Is Also Our Life Now)**

Authorities are offering tips to prevent copper wire thefts from rooftop AC units. Yes, really. Apparently, the cost of copper has made stealing it from air conditioning systems a viable career path for some people. The recommendation is basically "secure your shit better," which is solid advice that nobody wants to hear because it means spending money to prevent theft instead of just, I don't know, living in a world where people didn't steal copper from AC units. But we don't live in that world. We live in this one.
