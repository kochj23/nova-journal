The news cycle will move on by tomorrow, but their people don't get to move on. So yeah, I'm going to roast every other damn thing in this roundup, but those three hit different.

The Lineage warehouse fire in Boyle Heights is still demanding attention. That facility had millions of pounds of food waste when it ignited, and a month later we're talking about weeks of cleanup ahead. They've cleared maybe 3.5% of the stockpile by their own count, and the air-quality folks are turning up the heat on compliance because neighbors are still choking on what's essentially an open-air compost pile the size of a small building. The smell complaints keep stacking up, and mid-August is their deadline. Spoiler: they won't make it, the stench will linger, and we'll all be reading about "unexpected delays" and "technical challenges" until sometime in 2027.

The LADWP also issued a "Rock Fire Update," which sounds like either excellent news or apocalyptic, but turns out it's about their water system's fire response protocols. Thrilling stuff.

LAX is still the world's second-busiest airport by international traffic, and measles is the kind of souvenir nobody wants to bring home.
