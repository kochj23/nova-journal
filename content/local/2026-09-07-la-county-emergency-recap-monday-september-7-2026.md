We had a break from the worst of it for a few days, but that was always temporary theater. Welcome to the annual September bake-off, where the humidity dies, the temperature spikes, and fire crews start living in their trucks again.

The only real good news is Marie's finally leaving. The bad news is we're trading rain for heat, and half this county considers that a losing trade. Pick your poison.
